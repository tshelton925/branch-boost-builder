Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 17, 2026

Adding a spouse or relative, or keeping the home after a divorce, usually takes two steps. One is for the loan: a refinance or an approved assumption. The other is for the deed: a new deed recorded with the county. In Michigan, a deed change can also uncap taxable value unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses.

Key takeaways

  • The loan and the deed are separate. The deed shows who owns the home; the note shows who owes the money.
  • A quitclaim deed does not remove anyone from the loan. Only a refinance, a sale or the lender's written release does.
  • Federal rules stop a lender from calling the loan when a spouse or child joins the title of a home you live in.
  • Check Michigan's uncapping exemptions before you record. Spouses and certain relatives are exempt. Otherwise, a typical home's taxable value jumped about 50.5% the year after a sale in 2026.

Not legal or tax advice. Have a Michigan attorney review any deed or divorce judgment.

Can you add someone to a mortgage without refinancing?

Usually not to the loan. The borrowers signed the note and the lender approved them on their credit and income, so a new borrower means a new loan. You can often add someone to the deed alone, but that makes them an owner, not a borrower, and lender consent and Michigan tax rules still matter.

Most mortgages carry a due-on-sale clause: the lender can demand full payment if ownership changes without its consent, except for the family transfers federal rules protect (12 CFR 191.5). For anyone else, ask your servicer first. To add a borrower, the usual route is refinancing your Michigan mortgage.

What is the difference between the mortgage and the deed?

The deed shows who owns the home and is recorded with the county Register of Deeds. The promissory note shows who owes the money, and the mortgage pledges the home as security for it. A person can be on one and not the other, so each change needs its own paperwork.

A Michigan Register of Deeds records a deed only once it is acknowledged, usually before a notary (MCL 565.47). Michigan's short-form quitclaim deed passes whatever interest the signer has, with no promise about the title. See conveyance and deeds in real estate.

The deed also sets how you hold title (State Tax Commission Transfer of Ownership Guidelines):

  • Tenancy by the entireties: spouses only, with survivorship.
  • Joint tenancy: equal shares, with survivorship.
  • Tenancy in common: shares can differ, with no survivorship.

How do you add a spouse or family member to your mortgage?

To put them on the loan, refinance with both of you as borrowers; both apply and qualify on credit and income. To put them on title only, record a new deed. Federal rules generally bar the lender from calling the loan when a spouse or child joins the title of a home you live in.

That protection, from the Garn-St Germain Act, covers a one- to four-unit home the borrower lives in, when the new owner lives there too. It also covers a divorce decree that makes a spouse an owner and a living trust the borrower lives in. It does not add or release a borrower. Sharing costs? Does adding someone to the deed raise Michigan property taxes?

Not when an exemption applies. Adding a spouse is not a transfer of ownership, and since December 31, 2014 neither is deeding a home to a parent, sibling, child or grandchild. A joint tenancy that keeps an original owner on title is also exempt. Adding anyone else as a tenant in common can partly uncap taxable value.

The rules are in MCL 211.27a(7)(a), (b), (i) and (u); the relative exemption also covers your spouse's relatives. Confirm with your assessor before you record.

How do you remove an ex-spouse from a mortgage in Michigan?

A quitclaim deed takes an ex-spouse off the title but not off the loan. To end their liability, the spouse keeping the home refinances alone, sells, or, where the loan allows, assumes it with a release of liability. The judgment of divorce should say who keeps the home and when the loan must be refinanced.

The lender is not a party to the divorce, so the judgment does not change who owes the note. Fannie Mae calls a debt assigned by court order a contingent liability until the creditor releases the borrower (Selling Guide B3-6-05).

For taxes, a divorce deed made solely to end a tenancy by the entireties does not uncap taxable value, even if the judgment sets a price (State Tax Commission). Former spouses who keep owning together become tenants in common, and a later transfer between them can partly uncap it.

Can you take over the mortgage without refinancing after a divorce?

Sometimes, if the loan allows an assumption and the person keeping the home qualifies:

  • FHA: assumable after a creditworthiness review; the departing borrower can then be released (24 CFR 203.510).
  • VA: assumable with approval (38 CFR 36.4309); after a divorce, the veteran can ask VA for a release. The veteran's entitlement stays in use unless a qualified veteran substitutes theirs.
  • USDA: needs USDA's approval, and the original borrower stays liable (7 CFR 3555.256).
  • Conventional: most are fixed-rate and not assumable; Fannie Mae's adjustable-rate loans usually are (Selling Guide B2-1.4-02).

How does a divorce buyout refinance work?

You refinance in your own name and use part of the new loan to pay your former spouse their share of the equity. Some loan programs treat a buyout under a divorce decree differently from a standard cash-out refinance. You need the decree or settlement agreement, an appraisal and enough income to qualify on your own.

Fannie Mae treats a buyout of a co-owner under a written agreement, such as a divorce settlement, as a limited cash-out refinance if the home was jointly owned for at least 12 months before the new loan funds (Selling Guide B2-1.3-02).

What Michigan taxes and forms apply when a name changes on a deed?

A deed change can be a transfer of ownership. Unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses, taxable value resets the next year to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid. File a Property Transfer Affidavit and keep the Principal Residence Exemption in place.

It is the same reset that follows a sale (MCL 211.27a(3)), and Align Lending's 2026 Michigan Property Tax Uncapping Study puts the typical jump at about 50.5%. See how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale.

  1. Property Transfer Affidavit (Form 2766): the new owner files it with the assessor within 45 days of the transfer (MCL 211.27a(10)). Late filing on a principal residence can cost $5 a day, up to $200 (MCL 211.27b). File it, claiming the exemption, even when the transfer is exempt.
  2. Principal Residence Exemption (Form 2368): a new owner who lives there files by June 1 or November 1; an owner who moves out files Form 2602 within 90 days. See Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption.
  3. Transfer tax: none is owed on a deed between spouses creating or ending a tenancy by the entireties, a court-ordered transfer with no stated price or a joint tenancy with an existing owner (MCL 207.505, MCL 207.526). Only the state tax exempts a deed to a child or grandchild. See Michigan transfer tax.
  4. Recording fee: $30 per deed at the county Register of Deeds, whatever the page count (MCL 600.2567).

A refinance alone uncaps nothing and owes no transfer tax (MCL 211.27a(7)(j); MCL 207.505(d) and 207.526(d)).

What does it cost to add or remove a name?

A deed-only change costs the deed preparation, often by an attorney or title company, plus the $30 county recording fee. A refinance has closing costs, shown on your Loan Estimate. An assumption has servicer fees, and VA caps the processing fee on its loans. Compare the total cost with the benefit before you decide.

VA's cap is in 38 CFR 36.4313; VA also charges a funding fee on an assumption. A refinance replaces your current loan's terms, so compare the whole new loan, including refinancing closing costs.

Questions about changing names on a Michigan mortgage

Can you add someone to a mortgage loan?

Yes, usually by refinancing. The person you add applies with you and qualifies on credit and income, and the new loan pays off the old one. A deed alone adds an owner, not a borrower, and does not change who owes the loan.

Does a quitclaim deed remove you from the mortgage?

No. A quitclaim deed changes who owns the home, not who owes the loan. Everyone who signed the note stays liable until a refinance or sale pays it off, or the lender releases a borrower in writing. Plan both steps together.

Can my ex keep the house and the mortgage in my name?

Yes, if the judgment allows it, but you stay liable to the lender, and late payments still reach your credit report. Fannie Mae lets lenders leave a debt assigned by a divorce decree out of your debt-to-income ratio; other programs set their own rules. Ask your attorney about the judgment's refinance deadline.

Does adding a spouse to the deed uncap property taxes in Michigan?

No. Under Michigan law, a transfer between spouses, or a deed that only creates a tenancy by the entireties, is not a transfer of ownership, so taxable value stays capped. File a Property Transfer Affidavit claiming the spouse exemption to avoid a mistaken uncapping.

Can you assume a mortgage after a divorce?

Sometimes. FHA and VA loans can be assumed with approval, and the departing borrower can ask for a release of liability. A USDA assumption leaves the original borrower liable, and most conventional loans cannot be assumed. On a VA loan, the veteran's entitlement stays in use unless a qualified veteran assumes it.

Do you need a refinance to add a spouse to a VA loan?

Not to add them to the deed. VA's rule lets a spouse or child become a joint owner of the home without the loan being called. To add a spouse to the loan itself, expect a refinance; the program and the lender decide who can be added.

How long after a divorce do you have to refinance?

There is no general legal deadline. The judgment of divorce or settlement agreement sets one, if at all. The lender is not bound by it, so both borrowers stay on the loan until a refinance closes. If you need time to qualify alone, say so early.
