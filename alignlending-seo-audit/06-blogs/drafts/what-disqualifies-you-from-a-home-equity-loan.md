# What disqualifies you from a home equity loan or HELOC?

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 16, 2026

**The short answer.** If you own a Michigan home and want to borrow against it, the usual deal-breakers are too little equity under the lender's combined loan-to-value cap, a debt-to-income ratio that is too high, and credit history. In 2025, Michigan lenders denied about 30% of HELOC applications, against 15.5% for purchase loans. Most of this can be checked before you apply.

**Key takeaways**

- Michigan lenders denied 30.1% of decided home equity line of credit (HELOC) applications and 27.3% of home equity loans in 2025, against 15.5% for purchase loans ([Align's Michigan Mortgage Market Report](/research/michigan-mortgage-market-report/)).
- Combined loan-to-value (CLTV), not the equity on paper, sets the size of a line.
- Debt-to-income ratio and credit history were the reasons cited most on Michigan home equity denials in 2025.
- A land contract, unpaid property taxes or a listed home can stop a file regardless of credit.

## What disqualifies you from a home equity loan or HELOC?

Seven things stop most home equity applications: too little equity after the lender's combined loan-to-value cap, a high debt-to-income ratio, a low credit score or recent late payments, income that is unstable or hard to document, the property type or condition, title or lien problems, and a recent bankruptcy or foreclosure. Lenders weigh them together.

1. **[Not enough equity under the cap.](#how-much-equity-do-you-need-for-a-heloc)** Lenders never lend all of it.
2. **[A high debt-to-income ratio.](#what-debt-to-income-ratio-do-you-need-for-a-home-equity-loan)** The new payment counts too.
3. **[Credit score or recent late payments.](#what-credit-score-do-you-need-for-a-heloc-or-home-equity-loan)** History can outweigh the number.
4. **[Unstable or undocumented income.](#what-can-you-fix-before-you-apply-and-how-fast)** The lender must verify it.
5. **[The property type or condition.](#which-properties-and-ownership-situations-disqualify-a-home-equity-loan)** Some homes are excluded.
6. **[Title or lien problems.](#which-properties-and-ownership-situations-disqualify-a-home-equity-loan)** Other claims on the title come first.
7. **[A recent bankruptcy or foreclosure.](#does-a-recent-bankruptcy-or-foreclosure-disqualify-you)** Lenders set waiting periods.

## How often are home equity loans denied in Michigan?

In 2025, Michigan lenders denied 30.1% of the HELOC applications they decided and 27.3% of closed-end home equity loan applications, about twice the 15.5% share for purchase loans. Debt-to-income ratio and credit history were cited most, with collateral third (Align's Michigan Mortgage Market Report, 2025 HMDA data).

| Loan purpose, Michigan 2025 | Applications | Share denied |
|---|---|---|
| Home purchase | 150,394 | 15.5% |
| Refinance | 38,334 | 16.4% |
| Cash-out refinance | 45,672 | 31.2% |
| HELOC | 83,611 | 30.1% |
| Closed-end home equity loan | 33,651 | 27.3% |

Source: Align Lending, Michigan Mortgage Market Report, 2025, from FFIEC HMDA data.

Purchase applicants usually arrive preapproved, while home equity applicants often apply without a check first. Michigan owners still took out 66,247 home equity loans and lines in 2025, with medians of $85,000 for a line and $55,000 for a loan.

## How much equity do you need for a HELOC?

Enough to stay under the lender's combined loan-to-value cap. Combined loan-to-value (CLTV) is every mortgage on the home, the new line included, divided by the appraised value. Each lender and program sets its own cap, so the most you can borrow is the cap times the value, minus what you owe, not your full equity.

> **CLTV = (all mortgage balances + the new line) ÷ appraised value**

The CFPB's [home equity line of credit booklet](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_heloc-brochure.pdf) illustrates this with a 75% cap and notes that the lender also weighs your ability to repay. Many lenders cap combined borrowing around 80% to 90% [VERIFY: typical HELOC and home equity CLTV caps; no GSE or regulator source found. If none, cut the range and say "many lenders set their own caps"].

### Can you borrow 100% of your home equity?

Rarely: the cap always leaves the lender a cushion. Texas, where Align is also licensed, caps homestead debt at 80% in the [Texas Constitution](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/CN/htm/CN.16.htm). This guide covers Michigan.

### What if the appraisal comes in low?

A lower value shrinks the line, or ends the application if you already owe close to the cap. Some lenders order a [drive-by appraisal](/learn/drive-by-appraisal/), which looks at the outside only. Later, [12 CFR 1026.40](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1026/subpart-E/section-1026.40) lets a lender freeze or reduce a line while the value is significantly below the original appraisal.

## What debt-to-income ratio do you need for a home equity loan?

Your debt-to-income ratio (DTI) is your monthly debt payments, including the first mortgage and the new home equity payment, divided by your gross monthly income. Limits vary by lender. DTI was one of the two reasons cited most on denied Michigan home equity applications in 2025, so it is the number to check first.

See [how debt-to-income ratio is calculated](/learn/debt-to-income-ratio-dti/). On a HELOC, some lenders count a payment on the full line, not only what you draw [VERIFY: lender practice for HELOC qualifying payments; the CFPB booklet sets no rule], so a smaller line can be the fix. The often-quoted 43% is not a hard cap: 30% of Michigan purchase loans made in 2025 exceeded it (Align's report).

## What credit score do you need for a HELOC or home equity loan?

No federal rule sets a minimum credit score for a HELOC or home equity loan. Each lender sets its own, and it can change with the loan size and the CLTV. Recent late mortgage payments, collections or a fresh bankruptcy can weigh more than the score. Credit history was the other reason cited most in 2025.

### Can you get a home equity loan with a 520 credit score?

Unlikely with most home equity lenders. Rebuild first with [credit repair steps before you apply](/learn/credit-repair-strategies-for-home-buyers/), or compare a cash-out refinance, which follows its own program's credit rules.

## Which properties and ownership situations disqualify a home equity loan?

Lenders usually decline or restrict homes listed for sale, some investment properties, certain manufactured homes, homes in some trusts or estates, and homes bought on a land contract, where the buyer holds equitable title and the seller keeps the deed. Michigan adds property tax and assistance-lien checks.

- **Land contracts.** The seller keeps the deed until payoff, and lenders generally lend only to the owner of record, so refinance out first. See [how a Michigan land contract works](/learn/michigan-land-contract/).
- **Unpaid property taxes.** Taxes unpaid on March 1 after the levy year turn delinquent ([MCL 211.78a](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-78a)). A year later the home forfeits to the county treasurer ([MCL 211.78g](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-78g)). If a foreclosure judgment goes unpaid by the next March 31, the government takes title and mortgage liens are wiped out ([MCL 211.78k](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-78k)). So lenders want delinquent taxes paid first.
- **Assistance liens.** The Michigan State Housing Development Authority's [MI 10K DPA loan](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/pathway-to-housing/mi-10k-dpa-loan) is a second mortgage, repaid when the first mortgage is paid off, the home is sold or refinanced, or it stops being owner-occupied. Some lenders refuse to sit behind it [VERIFY: MSHDA and city DPA policy on new subordinate liens].
- **Contractor liens and listed homes.** An unpaid contractor's construction lien usually has to be released first. Fannie Mae requires a listed home to come off the market before a [cash-out refinance](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.3-03/cash-out-refinance-transactions) funds; many equity lenders decline listed homes outright.
- **Trusts, estates and property type.** Lenders differ on trusts, and a home in probate usually passes to the heirs first [VERIFY: lender trust and estate eligibility]. Some exclude manufactured homes titled as personal property, and some lend only on a primary residence.

## Does a recent bankruptcy or foreclosure disqualify you?

Usually, for a time. Lenders set waiting periods after a bankruptcy, foreclosure or short sale, measured from when it ended, and home equity lenders are often stricter than first-mortgage programs. A second lien is repaid only after the first mortgage if the home is sold, so the lender carries more risk.

For comparison, [Fannie Mae's Selling Guide](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-5.3-07/significant-derogatory-credit-events-waiting-periods-and-re-establishing-credit) sets a 4-year wait after a Chapter 7 bankruptcy for a conventional first mortgage, or 2 years with documented extenuating circumstances.

## What can you fix before you apply, and how fast?

Pay down revolving balances to lower your DTI, correct credit report errors, gather income documents, clear delinquent property taxes or title issues, and get a realistic value before you apply. Most of these show up in a first conversation, before any credit pull, so you can decide what to fix first.

| Problem | What to do | When it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| DTI too high | Pay down card balances; ask for a smaller line | Usually the next statement cycle |
| Credit report error | Dispute it with the bureau and the creditor | After the correction posts |
| Income hard to document | Gather tax returns, W-2s or pay stubs | When the file is complete |
| Title or lien issue | Refinance the land contract, settle the estate or release the lien | When the deed or release is recorded |

## If you are turned down, what are your options?

Read the adverse action notice first. Under [Regulation B](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1002/section-1002.9), a lender that turns down a completed application must tell you within 30 days and either state the specific reasons or explain how to ask for them within 60 days. Then compare a smaller line, a cash-out refinance, waiting to rebuild, or a personal loan.

[VERIFY: confirm 12 CFR 1002.9 is unchanged by the Regulation B amendments effective June 30, 2026.] A cash-out refinance replaces your first mortgage, so compare [HELOC vs. home equity loan vs. cash-out refinance](/refinance/cash-out-vs-home-equity/). ## Questions about home equity loan approval

### What will disqualify you from a home equity loan?

Usually too little equity under the lender's combined loan-to-value cap, a debt-to-income ratio above its limit, or credit history such as recent late payments. Title problems, the property type, unpaid property taxes and a recent bankruptcy can also stop a file.

### Can you borrow 100% of your home equity?

Rarely. Lenders cap the combined balance of every loan on the home at a share of its appraised value, so part of your equity stays unborrowed. That cushion protects the lender if values fall or the home has to be sold.

### What credit score do you need for a HELOC?

There is no federal minimum. Each lender sets its own, and it can change with the size of the line and the combined loan-to-value ratio. Lenders also weigh recent late payments, collections and bankruptcies, which can matter more than the score itself.

### Can you get a home equity loan with a 520 credit score?

It is unlikely. With a score that low, many home equity lenders decline whatever the equity. The realistic options are to rebuild credit first, starting with report errors and card balances, or to compare a cash-out refinance under its own program's rules.

### What is the downside of taking a home equity loan?

Your home secures the debt, so missed payments can lead to foreclosure. There are closing costs, most HELOCs have a variable rate, and federal rules let a lender freeze or reduce a line in set cases, such as a significant drop in the home's value.

### How long after buying a house can you get a HELOC?

It depends on the lender. Some will open a line soon after closing if the combined loan-to-value fits their cap; others want you to own the home for a set time. After a recent purchase, equity is thin, so the cap is the real limit.

### Can you get a HELOC on a house you are buying on a land contract in Michigan?

Usually not until you hold the deed. On a Michigan land contract, the seller keeps the deed until payoff, and most home equity lenders lend only to the owner of record. The usual path is to refinance out of the contract, then borrow later.
