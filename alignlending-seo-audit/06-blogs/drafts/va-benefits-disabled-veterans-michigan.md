# VA home loan and property tax benefits for disabled veterans in Michigan

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 16, 2026

<div class="al-answer">

If you are a Michigan veteran with a service-connected disability, two benefits can stack when you buy. VA charges no funding fee to veterans receiving, or entitled to, disability compensation. Michigan exempts the home of a veteran rated 100% permanently and totally disabled or individually unemployable, or who received VA adapted housing help, from property tax. Both need paperwork.

</div>

<div class="al-takeaways">

**Key takeaways**

- No VA funding fee for veterans receiving, or entitled to, service-connected disability compensation, on purchases and VA refinances.
- Michigan's MCL 211.7b exemption needs one of three VA determinations: 100% permanent and total, individually unemployable, or an adapted housing grant.
- File Form 5107 with your city or township assessor during the year you claim; since 2025, no yearly refiling.
- Without the exemption, the post-sale reset raised a typical Michigan home's taxable value about 50.5% in 2026, per [Align's 2026 Michigan Property Tax Uncapping Study](/research/michigan-property-tax-uncapping-study/).

</div>

## What VA home loan benefits do disabled veterans get?

Veterans with a service-connected disability get the same VA loan benefits as every eligible veteran, including no monthly mortgage insurance and no VA loan limit with full entitlement. Those receiving or entitled to disability compensation also pay no VA funding fee. Veterans with certain severe service-connected disabilities may also qualify for VA adapted housing grants.

VA sets these rules; a private lender makes the loan and decides on approval. See [VA loans in Michigan](/product-loans/va-loans/) and [VA loan benefits for every veteran](/learn/va-loan-benefits-for-veterans/).

| Benefit | Who gets it | VA source |
|---|---|---|
| No VA funding fee | Veterans receiving or entitled to disability compensation, and the groups below | [VA funding fee and loan closing costs](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/) |
| Funding fee refund | Veterans whose award is effective before the loan closed | Same page |
| Exemption on refinances | The same groups, on a VA streamline (IRRRL) or cash-out refinance | Same page |
| No monthly mortgage insurance; no VA loan limit with full entitlement | Every eligible veteran | [VA home loan entitlement and limits](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/) |
| Specially adapted housing (SAH) and special housing adaptation (SHA) grants; maximums reset each fiscal year | Veterans with certain permanent and total service-connected disabilities | [Disability housing grants for Veterans](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/disability-housing-grants/) |

## Who is exempt from the VA funding fee?

VA lists who is exempt: veterans receiving compensation for a service-connected disability, or entitled to it but receiving retirement or active-duty pay instead; surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation; service members with a proposed or memorandum rating before closing; and active-duty Purple Heart recipients. Your Certificate of Eligibility shows the exemption.

The exemption covers the fee on any VA-guaranteed loan, including later [refinancing a VA loan](/learn/benefits-of-refinancing-va-loan/). It must be on record by closing; a claim still pending at closing generally means the fee is charged. Not exempt, or checking? [Estimate my VA funding fee](/va-funding-fee-calculator/).

### Can you get the funding fee refunded if your rating comes later?

Possibly. VA's test is the effective date of your disability award, not the date of the decision. In VA's example, a June 15 closing with an award effective June 1 may qualify, and an award effective July 1 generally would not. Ask your VA Regional Loan Center (877-827-3702) or your servicer. Source: VA News, [VA home loan funding fee refunds: Why timing matters](https://news.va.gov/150990/va-home-loan-funding-fee-refunds-timing-matters/). [VERIFY: refund rule and phone number on the VA funding fee page; record the article's date.]

## Does Michigan exempt disabled veterans from property tax?

Yes. Under MCL 211.7b, the homestead of a qualifying disabled veteran is exempt from property taxes collected under Michigan's General Property Tax Act. Qualifying means a VA determination of 100% permanent and total disability, individual unemployability, or specially adapted housing assistance. An unremarried surviving spouse keeps it, even on a home bought later.

[MCL 211.7b](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-7b) also requires a Michigan resident discharged under honorable conditions. There is no income test and no partial exemption. Two details catch people, per the State Tax Commission's [Disabled Veterans Exemption FAQ](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/treasury/STC/2023/Disabled-Veterans-Exemption-FAQ-w-samples---Revised-1424.pdf) (updated June 13, 2024). A combined rating below 100% does not qualify unless VA has also rated you individually unemployable. And Dependency and Indemnity Compensation alone does not qualify a surviving spouse, although it does waive the VA funding fee.

| Rule | What Michigan law and the State Tax Commission say |
|---|---|
| Proof | A letter or certificate from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs; a county veterans office letter is not enough |
| Where and when to file | Form 5107 with the city or township assessor, after January 1 and before December 31 of the year claimed |
| Refiling | Not required for an exemption granted for taxes levied on or after January 1, 2025 |
| Who must hold title | The veteran alone or with a spouse; the surviving spouse alone |

Source: MCL 211.7b, the State Tax Commission FAQ, Form 5107 (Rev. 03-26) and Treasury's [Disabled Veteran Exemption](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/property/exemptions/homeowner/disabled-veteran-exemption) page.

### What does the exemption cover?

Property taxes under the General Property Tax Act on the one home you own and live in. Special assessments generally still apply. Vacant parcels next door are not covered, and business use other than farming disqualifies the home.

### Does a land contract or a home held in a trust qualify?

A trust qualifies only if its current beneficiaries are limited to the veteran and spouse, or the unremarried surviving spouse. Title must sit with the veteran alone or with a spouse, so adding a parent or adult child to the deed can cost the exemption. The FAQ is silent on land contracts, where the seller usually keeps title until payoff. [VERIFY: land contract buyers under MCL 211.7b(4)(b); ask the State Tax Commission.] Settle how you will take title before closing.

## How do you file Michigan's disabled veterans exemption?

File Form 5107, the State Tax Commission Affidavit for Disabled Veterans Exemption, with the assessor of the city or township where the home sits, along with the VA letter showing your qualifying determination. File after January 1 and before December 31 of the year you claim. Since 2025, a granted exemption stays in effect without refiling.

The assessor decides; boards of review no longer hear new claims. A December 30 postmark counts. For a new purchase, [Form 5107](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/treasury/Forms/Local-Government/PSD/STC/5107.pdf) (Rev. 03-26) asks for your closing documents, because the first year is prorated from them.

1. Get your VA benefit summary letter or grant certificate.
2. Complete Form 5107.
3. File it with the assessor, with the VA letter and closing documents.
4. Keep copies and the assessor's written decision.
5. Send the approval to your servicer and ask for a new escrow analysis.

## How do these benefits change your loan and budget in Michigan?

The funding fee exemption lowers your loan amount or cash to close, because there is no fee to finance or pay. The property tax exemption removes the tax line from your housing payment once the assessor grants it, which can change your debt-to-income ratio. Lenders may still escrow taxes until the exemption is on record.

[VERIFY: lender practice on escrow and the debt-to-income ratio (DTI) before the assessor grants the exemption.]

The year after a home sells, its taxable value resets to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid. Align's study puts the statewide median homestead millage at 36.0 mills (2025 rates), about 1.8% of market value a year after the reset. See [how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale](/learn/michigan-taxable-value/).

**Hypothetical example, not an offer. Actual rates, costs and payments vary.** A $280,000 purchase where the assessor's market value equals the price. Property tax only, at the statewide median millage; no loan, insurance, special assessments or first-year proration.

| Line | Without the exemption | With the exemption granted |
|---|---|---|
| Market value (assumed equal to the price) | $280,000 | $280,000 |
| Taxable value after the reset (half of market value) | about $140,000 | about $140,000 |
| Millage (statewide median, 2025) | 36.0 mills | Exempt |
| Property tax for a full year | about $5,040 | $0 |

Local millage varies, so [estimate your Michigan property tax after you buy](/michigan-property-tax-calculator/). The closing year is prorated, and prior-year taxes you paid the seller are not refunded. If escrow already paid a bill, ask your servicer about the refund.

## Do disabled veterans still need to meet VA credit and income rules?

Yes. The benefits change the cost of the loan, not the approval test. VA disability compensation counts as income, and because it is tax-free, VA's handbook lets lenders gross up verified non-taxable income for the debt-to-income ratio. The loan still has to meet VA's residual income standard and the lender's approval.

See VA's Lenders Handbook, [VA Pamphlet 26-7, chapter 4](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000330850/VA-Pamphlet-VAP26-7-Chapter-04-Credit-Underwriting). Residual income, what is left each month after major expenses, uses actual income. [VERIFY: chapter 4 gross-up and residual income rules as applied to disability compensation.] See [VA loan requirements](/learn/how-to-qualify-for-va-loan/) and [how to buy a home with your VA benefit](/learn/buying-home-with-va-loan/).

## What if you do not qualify for the full Michigan exemption?

Veterans below the MCL 211.7b standard still get Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption like any owner who lives in the home, which exempts it from up to 18 mills of school operating tax. Veterans with a service-connected disability may also claim the homestead property tax credit on Form MI-1040CR-2, with a larger allowance at higher ratings.

File the exemption affidavit by June 1 (summer levy) or November 1 (winter levy); see [Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption](/learn/homestead-exemption-benefits-and-filing-guide/). For the 2025 tax year, Treasury's [MI-1040CR-2 booklet](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/Forms/IIT/TY2025/MI-1040CR-2-Book.pdf) sets a taxable value allowance of $3,500 for ratings of 10% to 50%, $4,000 for 60% to 80% and $4,500 for 90% to 100%. No credit is allowed above $71,500 of total household resources or $165,400 of taxable value. Treasury says to file whichever of the MI-1040CR or MI-1040CR-2 gives the larger credit.

## Where can Michigan veterans get help with the paperwork?

The Michigan Veterans Affairs Agency connects veterans with accredited Veteran Service Officers, who help with VA letters and benefit claims, through 1-800-MICH-VET or its county search. Your city or township assessor handles Form 5107. For a rating decision or an appeal, work with a VA-accredited representative.

The [Michigan Veterans Affairs Agency](https://www.michigan.gov/mvaa) lists 1-800-MICH-VET, and its [Find a Veteran Service Officer](https://www.michigan.gov/mvaa/county-filter-search-locations) search lists officers by county. [VERIFY: whether MVAA or VA states this help is at no cost.]

## Questions about VA benefits for disabled veterans in Michigan

### Who is exempt from paying the VA funding fee?

Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt, as are veterans entitled to it who take retirement or active-duty pay instead. So are surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, service members with a proposed or memorandum rating before closing, and active-duty Purple Heart recipients.

### Do disabled veterans pay property tax in Michigan?

Not if they qualify under MCL 211.7b. A Michigan veteran rated 100% permanently and totally disabled or individually unemployable, or who received a VA adapted housing grant, owes no property tax on the homestead once the assessor grants the exemption. Special assessments may still apply.

### What disability rating do you need for the Michigan property tax exemption?

You need one of three service-connected VA determinations: permanent and total disability at the 100% level, individual unemployability, or a specially adapted housing or special housing adaptation grant. A combined rating below 100% does not qualify by itself, and no income or asset test applies.

### Do you have to file Form 5107 every year?

No, not since 2025. An exemption granted for taxes levied on or after January 1, 2025 stays in effect until you rescind it or the assessor denies it. File Form 6054 within 45 days if you sell, move out or stop qualifying.

### Can a surviving spouse keep the Michigan disabled veterans exemption?

Yes, while the spouse stays unremarried, if the veteran qualified immediately before death. It follows the spouse to any Michigan homestead the spouse owns, including a later purchase. Dependency and Indemnity Compensation alone does not qualify a spouse for it.

### Can you get a VA funding fee refund after a disability rating?

Sometimes. VA bases a refund on the effective date of your disability award. If that date falls before your loan closed, you may qualify even though the decision came later. If it falls after closing, you generally do not.

### Does a 100% disabled veteran need a down payment for a VA loan?

VA does not require a down payment from an eligible veteran, disabled or not, with full entitlement who buys at or below the appraised value, according to VA's [entitlement and loan limits page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/). Lenders may set their own rules, and a low appraisal can mean bringing cash to closing.
