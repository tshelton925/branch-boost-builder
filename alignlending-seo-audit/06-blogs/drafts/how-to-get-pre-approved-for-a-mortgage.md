# How to get preapproved for a mortgage in Michigan: steps, documents and timeline

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 16, 2026

To get preapproved for a mortgage, you share your income, assets and debts with a lender, authorize a credit check and send documents the lender reviews before it issues a conditional letter. Michigan homebuyers should check one more thing: that the lender figured your payment with the new owner's property tax, which can run well above the seller's bill.

**Key takeaways**

- **6 steps:** check credit and budget, gather documents, choose a lender or broker, apply, answer underwriting questions, receive the letter.
- **Core documents:** a pay stub dated within 30 days of applying, W-2s for the last 1 or 2 years, the 2 most recent monthly bank statements, photo ID and a list of debts (Fannie Mae).
- **The credit check:** usually a hard inquiry; the CFPB says mortgage credit checks within a 45-day window count as one.
- **Letters expire:** lenders set the date, often tied to how old your credit report and pay stubs are; Fannie Mae allows credit documents up to 4 months old on the day you close.

Still deciding whether you need a letter at all? Start with [why preapproval matters](/learn/importance-of-mortgage-preapproval/).

## How do you get preapproved for a mortgage, step by step?

You get preapproved in 6 steps: check your credit and budget, gather your documents, choose a lender or broker, apply and authorize the credit check, answer the underwriter's questions, and receive the letter. A real preapproval rests on documents someone has reviewed. A letter based only on what you typed into a form is a prequalification.

1. **Check your credit and set your budget.** Look for errors on your credit reports first; [the CFPB explains how to get free copies and dispute mistakes](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/). Pick a monthly payment you are comfortable with, taxes and insurance included.
2. **Gather your documents.** Collect pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank statements, photo ID and a list of debts. The table below covers self-employment, retirement income and gifts.
3. **Choose a lender or broker.** A bank or credit union offers its own programs; an independent mortgage broker compares several wholesale lenders. Ask each what its letter verifies.
4. **Apply and authorize the credit check.** Once a lender has your name, income, Social Security number, a property address, an estimated value and the loan amount, federal rules require a [Loan Estimate within 3 business days](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1026/subpart-C/section-1026.19).
5. **Answer underwriting questions.** Expect requests about large deposits, employment gaps or recent credit inquiries. Answer with documents where you can.
6. **Receive the letter.** It names a loan amount and type and lists conditions, such as an appraisal. Loan approval is subject to underwriting, credit, income, asset, appraisal and property eligibility review.

For everything from application to closing day, see [our mortgage process, step by step](/mortgage-process/).

## What documents do you need to get preapproved?

Most buyers need recent pay stubs, W-2s for the last 1 or 2 years, the 2 most recent monthly bank statements, photo ID and details on their debts. Self-employed borrowers usually add 2 years of personal and business tax returns, or 12 to 24 months of bank statements for a bank statement loan. Gift funds need a signed gift letter.

| Document | W-2 employee | Self-employed | Retirement income | Gift funds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photo ID | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Income | Pay stub dated within 30 days of applying, with year-to-date earnings; W-2s for the most recent 1 or 2 years | If you own 25% or more of the business: usually 2 years of signed personal and business tax returns, sometimes 1 year; bank statement loans use 12 to 24 months of statements | Social Security or pension award letter, or distribution statements | Not income; a gift counts as an asset |
| Assets | 2 most recent monthly statements (or the latest quarterly) per account | Same, plus business statements if business funds are used | Same | Signed gift letter stating no repayment is expected, plus proof of transfer |
| Debts | Debts not on your credit report, such as child support | Same | Same | Same |

Source: Fannie Mae Selling Guide for conventional loans: [employment and income documentation](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.2-01/standards-employment-and-income-documentation), [deposits and assets](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-4.2-01/verification-deposits-and-assets), [income](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3/income-assessment) and [personal gifts](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-4.3-04/personal-gifts). FHA, VA and USDA loans follow their own agency rules, bank statement loans follow each lender's non-QM guidelines, and any lender can ask for more.

Self-employed? The [self-employed homebuyer plan](/purchase/self-employed-homebuyer/) explains how lenders read business income. For a printable list, download Align's [Mortgage Pre-Approval Checklist (PDF)](https://alignlending.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Pre-Approval-checklist.pdf).

## Does getting preapproved hurt your credit?

Usually only a little. A preapproval normally includes a hard credit inquiry, which can lower a score slightly. The CFPB says multiple mortgage credit checks within a 45-day window count as one inquiry, so comparing lenders in that window costs about the same as applying once. Talking with a lender or broker first needs no credit pull.

The window comes from credit-scoring models, not federal law ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-exactly-happens-when-a-mortgage-lender-checks-my-credit-en-2005/)). Keeping your mortgage applications close together keeps the effect small.

For program minimums, see [what credit score you need](/learn/whats-good-credit-score-to-buy-house/).

## What credit score and income do you need to get preapproved?

There is no single number. FHA, VA, USDA and conventional programs each set credit rules or leave minimums to lenders. For conventional loans, Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter (DU) evaluates credit without a fixed 620 floor for case files created on or after November 16, 2025, though lenders may add overlays. Income counts through your debt-to-income ratio (DTI).

Fannie Mae announced the change in [Selling Guide Announcement SEL-2025-09](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/news-events/announcement-sel-2025-09-selling-guide-updates). An overlay is a lender's own rule added to the agency's, so two lenders can answer the same file differently. For FHA loans, [HUD Handbook 4000.1](https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/single-family-handbook-4000-1) sets the minimum credit score at 500, with 580 or higher for maximum financing.

Your DTI is your total monthly debt payments divided by your gross monthly income, and property tax counts in the housing payment. See [debt-to-income limits by loan type](/learn/debt-to-income-ratio-dti/).

## How long does preapproval take, and how long does a letter last?

Once your documents are in, preapproval can take anywhere from the same day to a few business days, depending on how complete the file is and how many questions come up. The lender sets the letter's expiration date. Your pay stubs, bank statements and credit report must still be current when you close, so expect to send updates.

For conventional loans, Fannie Mae limits credit documents, including the credit report, pay stubs and bank statements, to 4 months old on the note date, the day you sign at closing ([allowable age of credit documents](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b1-1-03/allowable-age-credit-documents-and-federal-income-tax-returns)). Lenders often set a letter's expiration on a similar schedule, so a long search usually means new pay stubs, statements and sometimes a new credit report. No one can promise a timeline before seeing the file.

## Is there such a thing as instant mortgage preapproval?

Not in the way that matters to a seller. An instant online result is a prequalification based on what you typed, not a review of your documents. The CFPB notes that lenders use these terms differently, so ask what each letter verified. Use online tools to estimate, then get a document-backed preapproval before you make an offer.

A quick prequalification is useful for a price range. The [CFPB says neither kind of letter is a guaranteed loan offer](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/whats-the-difference-between-a-prequalification-letter-and-a-preapproval-letter-en-127/). Michigan sellers and their agents generally expect a preapproval backed by reviewed documents. Compare the two in [prequalified vs. preapproved](/learn/prequalified-vs-preapproved-whats-difference/).

## Where should you get preapproved: a bank, a credit union or a broker?

No single place fits everyone. Compare a bank, a credit union and an independent mortgage broker on the loan programs each can offer, the Loan Estimate each gives you and how clearly each explains your options. A bank or credit union offers its own programs. A broker compares several wholesale lenders on one application.

## What should Michigan buyers know about preapproval?

Ask the lender to budget with the new owner's property tax, because the year after a home sells, its taxable value resets to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid. Your purchase agreement usually sets a financing deadline, and Michigan State Housing Development Authority (MSHDA) loans require a participating lender.

Align Lending's [2026 Michigan Property Tax Uncapping Study](/research/michigan-property-tax-uncapping-study/) puts the typical taxable value jump the year after a sale at about 50.5% statewide, and the median homestead rate at 36.0 mills (2025 rates), about 1.8% of market value a year after the reset. Before you ask for a letter, [estimate your Michigan property tax after you buy](/michigan-property-tax-calculator/).

| Financing contingency date | A purchase agreement with a mortgage contingency usually sets a date for loan approval [VERIFY: confirm against the mortgage-contingency clause of the current Michigan Realtors Buy and Sell Agreement, or with a Michigan real estate attorney] | "Can my file be approved by this date?" |

| Letter amount | The letter can show your offer price, not your maximum | "Can you write it for my offer price?" |

## What can void a preapproval after you have the letter?

A preapproval can change or be canceled if your file changes after the letter: new debt, a job change, large deposits you cannot document, missed payments, or a change in the price or loan type. Until closing, tell your loan officer before you make any financial change, including opening a card or moving money between accounts.

- **New debt:** a car loan, card or financed furniture adds a payment and can push your DTI past the limit.
- **A job change:** income must be verified again, and lenders reconfirm employment shortly before closing.
- **Undocumented deposits:** on a purchase, a large deposit you need for closing must have a documented source.
- **Missed payments:** a new late payment can lower your score.
- **A different home or loan:** a higher price or another program means a new review.

## Questions about mortgage preapproval

### How long does it take to get preapproved for a mortgage?

It depends on the file. With complete documents, a preapproval can come back the same day or within a few business days. Self-employment income, gift funds, missing pages or credit questions add time, because each needs a document and a review.

### Does preapproval hurt your credit score?

Slightly. The hard inquiry in a preapproval can lower a score a little, and the CFPB says multiple mortgage credit checks within a 45-day window count as one inquiry. A conversation with a lender or broker before you apply needs no credit pull.

### What disqualifies you from getting preapproved?

Credit history and debt are the reasons lenders give most often. On denied Michigan purchase applications in 2025, lenders cited credit history and debt-to-income ratio each 42% of the time, and not enough cash to close 22%, according to Align Lending's Michigan Mortgage Market Report (2025 HMDA data).

### Can you get preapproved with more than one lender?

Yes. Mortgage credit checks inside a short shopping window count as one inquiry for scoring. Each lender with a full application, including a property address, must send a Loan Estimate within 3 business days, so you can compare costs on the same federal form.

### How much house can I get preapproved for?

A lender preapproves you up to a maximum based on your income, debts, credit and cash, with taxes and insurance in the payment. That maximum is not a spending target. Align's budget review and the home affordability calculator help you set a comfortable monthly number first.

### Do I need a preapproval to make an offer in Michigan?

Not by law, but most sellers and listing agents expect one. A preapproval backed by reviewed documents shows that a lender has already looked at your income, assets and credit. That matters because Michigan purchase agreements usually set a date by which your financing must be approved.

### What is the difference between prequalification and preapproval?

A prequalification is an estimate based on what you tell a lender, usually without documents. A preapproval rests on reviewed documents and a credit check. Lenders use the terms differently, so ask what each letter verified. Align's prequalified vs. preapproved guide covers the rest.
