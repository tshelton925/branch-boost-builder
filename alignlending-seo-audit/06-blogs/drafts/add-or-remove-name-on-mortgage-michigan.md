# Adding or removing a name on a mortgage or deed in Michigan

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 16, 2026

<!-- .al-answer -->
Adding a spouse or relative, or keeping the home after a divorce, usually takes two separate steps: one for the loan (a refinance or a lender-approved assumption) and one for the deed (a new deed recorded with the county). In Michigan, a deed change can also uncap taxable value unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses.

<!-- .al-takeaways -->
**Key takeaways**

- **The loan and the deed are separate.** The deed, recorded with the county Register of Deeds, shows who owns the home. The note and mortgage show who owes the money.
- **A quitclaim deed does not remove anyone from the loan.** Everyone who signed the note stays liable until a refinance or sale pays it off, or the lender releases them in writing.
- **Federal law protects certain family transfers.** The Garn-St Germain Act bars a lender from calling the loan due when a spouse or child becomes an owner, or a divorce decree gives the home to the borrower's spouse, on a home with fewer than five units `[VERIFY: 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d)]`.
- **Check Michigan's uncapping exemptions before you record a deed.** Transfers between spouses, and since December 31, 2014 certain transfers of a home to a parent, sibling, child or grandchild, do not uncap taxable value (MCL 211.27a(7)). In 2026, a typical Michigan home's taxable value rose about 50.5% the year after a sale.

*This guide is general information, not legal or tax advice. Deeds, trusts and divorce judgments are legal documents. Have a Michigan real estate or family law attorney prepare or review them.*

## Can you add someone to a mortgage without refinancing?

<!-- .al-answer -->
Usually not to the loan itself. The original borrowers signed the note, and the lender approved them on their own credit and income, so a new borrower normally means a new loan. You can often add someone to the deed without changing the loan. That makes them an owner, not a borrower, and the lender's consent and Michigan tax rules still matter.

Read the transfer section of your mortgage. Most include a due-on-sale clause that lets the lender demand full payment if ownership changes without its consent `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac Michigan uniform security instrument, Form 3023]`. Federal law takes certain family transfers off the table, covered below. Adding someone who is not your spouse or child, such as a partner or a parent, is not on that federal list, so ask your servicer in writing before you record the deed. If the goal is a second borrower, the usual route is [refinancing your Michigan mortgage](/refinance/) into both names.

| Change | How it is done | Who owes the loan | Lender approval needed? | Michigan tax effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Add to deed only | New deed signed by the current owners, recorded with the county | The original borrowers only | Not for a spouse or child on a home you live in; ask first for anyone else `[VERIFY]` | None for a spouse, a listed relative or a joint tenancy with an original owner; a tenancy-in-common share can partly uncap |
| Add to loan | Refinance in both names | Everyone who signs the new note | Yes, the new lender underwrites both of you | None from the refinance itself |
| Assumption | Servicer approves a borrower to take over the existing loan | The person assuming; the original borrower too, unless released | Yes; FHA, VA and USDA loans with approval, most conventional loans not `[VERIFY]` | Depends on the deed that goes with it |
| Remove from deed | Departing owner signs a quitclaim deed, recorded with the county | Unchanged: everyone who signed the note | Not for a transfer to a spouse or under a divorce decree `[VERIFY]` | None between spouses or to end a tenancy by the entireties in a divorce |
| Remove from loan | Refinance, assumption with release of liability, or sale | The remaining borrower, or no one after a sale | Yes | None from the refinance itself |

*General rules from 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3, MCL 211.27a and the State Tax Commission's Transfer of Ownership Guidelines (revised February 25, 2025). Not legal advice.*

## What is the difference between the mortgage and the deed?

<!-- .al-answer -->
The deed shows who owns the home, and it is recorded with the county Register of Deeds. The promissory note shows who owes the money, and the mortgage pledges the home as security for that debt. A person can be on one and not the other, so each change needs its own paperwork.

A Michigan Register of Deeds will not record a deed unless it is acknowledged, usually before a notary ([MCL 565.47](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-565-47)), and meets the state's format rules, such as each signer's name printed under the signature and the new owner's address ([MCL 565.201](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-565-201)). A warranty deed promises clear title ([MCL 565.151](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-565-151)). A quitclaim deed passes whatever interest the signer has, with no promise about the title `[VERIFY: MCL 565.152]`. Neither one changes the note. For the vocabulary, see [conveyance and deeds in real estate](/learn/conveyance-in-real-estate/).

The deed's wording also sets how you hold title, according to the State Tax Commission's [Transfer of Ownership Guidelines](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/STC/TransferOwnershipGuidelines.pdf):

- **Tenancy by the entireties** exists only between spouses. A deed to a married couple creates one unless it says otherwise, and the survivor owns the whole home.
- **Joint tenancy** gives equal shares with survivorship, and the deed has to say so.
- **Tenancy in common** allows unequal shares and has no survivorship. A deed to two unmarried people with no other wording creates one.

## How do you add a spouse or family member to your mortgage?

<!-- .al-answer -->
To put them on the loan, refinance with both of you as borrowers, which means both of you apply and qualify on credit and income. To put them on title only, record a new deed. Under federal law, adding a spouse or child to the title of a home you live in generally does not let the lender call the loan due.

The [Garn-St Germain Act](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701j-3&num=0&edition=prelim) lists transfers that cannot trigger a due-on-sale clause on a home with fewer than five units. The ones that come up when names change: a transfer where the borrower's spouse or children become owners; a transfer under a divorce decree or property settlement by which the spouse becomes an owner; a transfer into a living trust in which the borrower stays a beneficiary; and a transfer on the death of a joint tenant `[VERIFY: 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d)(3), (6), (7), (8)]`. The federal rule applies these exceptions to homes the borrower occupies ([12 CFR 191.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-191/section-191.5)) `[VERIFY: 191.5(b)]`.

The protection is narrow. It stops the lender from calling the loan. It does not make the new owner a borrower, and it does not release anyone already on the note.

On the loan side, the lender looks at each applicant's credit, income, debts and documents, whoever they are to you. If the person joining will share the costs, the questions about shares and exits are the same ones that come up when [co-buying a home with family or friends](/learn/co-buying-home-with-family-or-friends/).

### Does adding someone to the deed raise Michigan property taxes?

Not when an exemption applies. Michigan does not treat adding a spouse as a transfer of ownership, and since December 31, 2014 the same is true for a home deeded to a parent, sibling, child or grandchild. Creating a joint tenancy that keeps an original owner on title is also exempt. Adding anyone else as a tenant in common can partly uncap taxable value.

The rules come from [MCL 211.27a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-27a) and the State Tax Commission's guidelines:

- **Spouse:** a transfer from one spouse to the other, or a deed made only to create a tenancy by the entireties, is not a transfer of ownership (MCL 211.27a(7)(a) and (b)).
- **Listed relatives:** a transfer of a home to your or your spouse's mother, father, brother, sister, son, daughter, adopted child, grandson or granddaughter is exempt if the home is not used for a commercial purpose afterward (MCL 211.27a(7)(u)). The assessor can ask for proof, due within 30 days.
- **Joint tenancy:** creating one is exempt when an original owner, or that owner's spouse, stays on as a joint tenant, even if the new joint tenant is not a relative (MCL 211.27a(7)(i)).
- **Tenancy in common:** conveying a share is a transfer for that share, so a partial uncapping can follow the next year.

The commission tells assessors to read these exemptions strictly against the person claiming them. Confirm the result with your city or township assessor before you record.

## How do you remove an ex-spouse from a mortgage in Michigan?

<!-- .al-answer -->
A quitclaim deed takes an ex-spouse off the title but not off the loan. To end their liability, the spouse keeping the home refinances in their own name, gets a lender-approved assumption with a release of liability, or sells. The judgment of divorce should say who keeps the home and by when the loan must be refinanced.

The lender is not a party to the divorce. A judgment can assign the mortgage to one spouse, but the note still binds everyone who signed it until it is paid off or the lender releases someone `[VERIFY: confirm wording with a Michigan family law attorney]`. Until then, a late payment can affect both borrowers' credit.

Michigan's tax rules turn on timing. A deed between spouses, or one made as part of the divorce solely to end a tenancy by the entireties, does not uncap taxable value, according to the State Tax Commission. Former spouses who keep owning the home together after the divorce hold it as tenants in common, and a later transfer between them can partly uncap taxable value. Ask your attorney how the deed's timing fits the judgment.

If keeping the home does not work, compare it with [selling a home during a divorce](/learn/selling-home-during-divorce/). If the person leaving signed as a co-signer rather than an owner, see [refinancing to remove a co-signer](/learn/refinancing-to-remove-co-signer/).

### Can you take over the mortgage without refinancing after a divorce?

Sometimes, through an assumption. FHA, VA and USDA loans can generally be assumed with the servicer's approval when the person keeping the home qualifies, and the departing borrower should ask for a written release of liability. Most conventional loans cannot be assumed. Either way, the spouse keeping the home qualifies on their own `[VERIFY]`.

On a VA loan, the veteran's entitlement can stay tied to the loan after an assumption unless the person assuming is an eligible veteran who substitutes their own entitlement, which matters when the veteran is the spouse who leaves ([38 U.S.C. 3714](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3714&num=0&edition=prelim)) `[VERIFY]`. An FHA release depends on the servicer's review of the remaining borrower ([HUD Handbook 4000.1](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/4000.1hsgh.pdf)) `[VERIFY]`, and a USDA assumption needs approval ([7 CFR 3555.256](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/subtitle-B/chapter-XXXV/part-3555/subpart-F/section-3555.256)) `[VERIFY]`.

## How does a divorce buyout refinance work?

<!-- .al-answer -->
You refinance in your own name and use part of the new loan to pay your former spouse their share of the equity. Some loan programs treat a buyout under a divorce decree differently from a standard cash-out refinance. You need the decree or settlement agreement, an appraisal and enough income to qualify on your own.

Fannie Mae, for example, lets a buyout of a co-owner's equity under a written agreement close as a limited cash-out refinance when the home has been jointly owned for at least 12 months ([Selling Guide B2-1.3-02](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.3-02/limited-cash-out-refinance-transactions)) `[VERIFY: current edition]`. FHA has its own rules `[VERIFY: HUD 4000.1, divorce equity buyout]`. For uncapping, the State Tax Commission says a divorce judgment that orders one spouse to pay the other a set amount for the home is generally not a transfer of ownership, because the deed ends a tenancy by the entireties.

The new loan still has to fit the program's limit on loan-to-value ratio (LTV), the loan amount divided by the appraised value. ## What Michigan taxes and forms apply when a name changes on a deed?

<!-- .al-answer -->
A deed change can be a transfer of ownership that uncaps taxable value the next year, resetting it to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses. The new owner files a Property Transfer Affidavit with the assessor, and the home's Principal Residence Exemption should stay in place.

The year after a home sells, its taxable value resets to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid. A deed change that is not exempt triggers the same reset (MCL 211.27a(3)). In 2026, the reset raised a typical Michigan home's taxable value about 50.5% the year after a sale, according to Align Lending's [2026 Michigan Property Tax Uncapping Study](/research/michigan-property-tax-uncapping-study/). For the mechanics, see [how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale](/learn/michigan-taxable-value/).

1. **Property Transfer Affidavit ([Form 2766](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/treasury/Forms/Local-Government/2766.pdf)).** The new owner files it with the city or township assessor within 45 days of the transfer (MCL 211.27a(10)). On a principal residence, a late filing can cost $5 a day, up to $200. The commission encourages filing even when an exemption applies, so check the exemption box and the assessor does not uncap the home by mistake.
2. **Principal Residence Exemption ([Form 2368](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/Forms/Local-Government/2368.pdf)).** It exempts the home you live in from up to 18 mills of school operating tax ([Michigan Treasury](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/property/principal)). An owner who lives there files by June 1 for that year's summer and winter levies, or by November 1 for the winter levy. An owner who no longer owns or lives in the home files [Form 2602](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/Forms/Local-Government/2602.pdf) within 90 days. More on [Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption](/learn/homestead-exemption-benefits-and-filing-guide/).
3. **Transfer tax.** State and county transfer taxes are due when a deed is recorded unless an exemption applies. Both acts exempt a deed between spouses that creates or ends a tenancy by the entireties, a court-ordered transfer with no stated dollar amount, a deed creating a joint tenancy with someone who already owns the home, and a deed for less than $100 ([MCL 207.505](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-207-505), [MCL 207.526](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-207-526)). Only the state act exempts a deed to a child, stepchild or grandchild. Refunds go through [Michigan Treasury](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/business-taxes/misc/state-real-estate-transfer-tax-srett). More on [Michigan transfer tax](/learn/michigan-transfer-tax/).
4. **Recording fee.** The county Register of Deeds charges a fee to record the deed `[VERIFY: current fee, MCL 600.2567]`.

A refinance on its own uncaps nothing, because a mortgage is a security interest, not a transfer of ownership (MCL 211.27a(7)(j)), and the state act exempts it from transfer tax (MCL 207.526(d)).

## When should you talk to an attorney first?

<!-- .al-answer -->
Talk to a Michigan attorney before you record any deed during a divorce, an estate plan or a family transfer. The deed type, the form of ownership and the timing affect who is liable, what is taxed and who inherits. A mortgage broker can explain the loan side, not the legal one.

Bring the current deed, the latest mortgage statement and any draft judgment, trust or agreement. A family law attorney handles the judgment, a real estate attorney can prepare the deed, and a tax professional can answer gift or capital gains questions. Two Michigan points are worth raising: a tenancy by the entireties exists only between spouses and becomes a tenancy in common at divorce, and a deed to two people who later marry does not turn into one (State Tax Commission guidelines).

## Questions about changing names on a Michigan mortgage

### Can you add someone to a mortgage loan?

Yes, usually by refinancing. The person you add applies with you and qualifies on credit and income, and the new loan pays off the old one. Some FHA, VA and USDA loans can instead be assumed by a qualified borrower with the servicer's approval. Adding someone to the deed alone makes them an owner, not a borrower.

### Does a quitclaim deed remove you from the mortgage?

No. A quitclaim deed changes who owns the home, not who owes the loan. Everyone who signed the note stays liable until a refinance or sale pays it off, or the lender releases a borrower in writing, for example after an approved assumption. In a divorce, plan the deed and the refinance together.

### Can my ex keep the house and the mortgage in my name?

Yes, if the judgment allows it, but you stay liable to the lender. A late payment can affect your credit, and the debt can count against you when you apply for your next loan. A judgment can set a deadline for your ex to refinance or sell, so ask your attorney what happens if it passes.

### Does adding a spouse to the deed uncap property taxes in Michigan?

No. Michigan law says a transfer from one spouse to the other, or a deed that only creates a tenancy by the entireties, is not a transfer of ownership, so taxable value stays capped. File a Property Transfer Affidavit and check the spouse exemption so the assessor does not uncap the home by mistake.

### Can you assume a mortgage after a divorce?

Sometimes. FHA, VA and USDA loans can generally be assumed with the servicer's approval if the person keeping the home qualifies, while most conventional loans cannot. Ask for a written release of liability for the spouse who leaves. On a VA loan, the veteran's entitlement can stay tied to the loan unless an eligible veteran substitutes theirs.

### Do you need a refinance to add a spouse to a VA loan?

Not to add them to the deed. Federal law lets a borrower's spouse become an owner of a home the borrower lives in without the lender calling the loan due. To add a spouse to the loan itself, expect a refinance, and ask the lender which VA refinance options allow a borrower to be added.

### How long after a divorce do you have to refinance?

There is no general legal deadline. The judgment of divorce or settlement agreement sets one, if it sets one at all. The lender is not bound by the judgment, so both borrowers stay on the loan until a refinance closes. If you may need time to qualify alone, raise that with your attorney before the judgment is final.
