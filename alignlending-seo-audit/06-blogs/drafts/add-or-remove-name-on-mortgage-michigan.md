# Adding or removing a name on a mortgage or deed in Michigan

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 16, 2026

<!-- .al-answer -->
Adding a spouse or relative, or keeping the home after a divorce, usually takes two separate steps: one for the loan (a refinance or a lender-approved assumption) and one for the deed (a new deed recorded with the county). In Michigan, a deed change can also uncap taxable value unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses.

<!-- .al-takeaways -->
**Key takeaways**

- **The loan and the deed are separate.** The deed shows who owns the home. The note and mortgage show who owes the money.
- **A quitclaim deed does not remove anyone from the loan.** Only a refinance, a sale or the lender's written release does.
- **Federal law protects some family transfers.** Adding a spouse or child to title, or a divorce decree giving the home to a spouse, generally cannot trigger the due-on-sale clause `[VERIFY: 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d)]`.
- **Check Michigan's uncapping exemptions before you record.** Transfers between spouses, and since December 31, 2014 a home deeded to certain relatives, do not uncap taxable value (MCL 211.27a(7)). In 2026, a typical home's taxable value rose about 50.5% the year after a sale.

*General information, not legal or tax advice. Have a Michigan real estate or family law attorney prepare or review any deed or divorce judgment.*

## Can you add someone to a mortgage without refinancing?

<!-- .al-answer -->
Usually not to the loan. The original borrowers signed the note, and the lender approved them on their credit and income, so a new borrower means a new loan. You can often add someone to the deed without changing the loan, but that makes them an owner, not a borrower, and lender consent and Michigan tax rules still matter.

Most mortgages include a due-on-sale clause that lets the lender demand full payment if ownership changes without its consent `[VERIFY: Michigan uniform security instrument, Form 3023]`. For anyone other than a spouse or child, ask your servicer in writing before you record a deed. To add a borrower, the usual route is [refinancing your Michigan mortgage](/refinance/) into both names.

| Change | How | Who owes the loan | Lender approval? | Michigan tax effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Add to deed only | New deed, recorded with the county | Original borrowers only | Not for a spouse or child on your home; ask for anyone else | None for a spouse, listed relative or joint tenancy with an original owner |
| Add to loan | Refinance in both names | Everyone on the new note | Yes | None |
| Assumption | Servicer approves the borrower taking over | The new borrower, and the old one unless released | Yes; FHA, VA and USDA allow it with approval `[VERIFY]` | Depends on the deed |
| Remove from deed | Quitclaim deed, recorded | Unchanged | Not for a spouse or under a divorce decree | None between spouses or when ending a tenancy by the entireties in a divorce |
| Remove from loan | Refinance, assumption with release, or sale | The remaining borrower | Yes | None from a refinance |

*Based on 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3, MCL 211.27a and State Tax Commission guidelines. General rules, not legal advice.*

## What is the difference between the mortgage and the deed?

<!-- .al-answer -->
The deed shows who owns the home and is recorded with the county Register of Deeds. The promissory note shows who owes the money, and the mortgage pledges the home as security for it. A person can be on one and not the other, so each change needs its own paperwork.

A Michigan Register of Deeds records a deed only after it is acknowledged, usually before a notary ([MCL 565.47](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-565-47)). A quitclaim deed passes whatever interest the signer has, with no promise about title `[VERIFY: MCL 565.152]`. No deed changes the note. See [conveyance and deeds in real estate](/learn/conveyance-in-real-estate/).

The deed's wording also sets how you hold title, according to the State Tax Commission's [Transfer of Ownership Guidelines](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/STC/TransferOwnershipGuidelines.pdf):

- **Tenancy by the entireties:** spouses only, with survivorship. A deed to a married couple creates one unless it says otherwise.
- **Joint tenancy:** equal shares with survivorship, stated on the deed.
- **Tenancy in common:** shares can differ, with no survivorship.

## How do you add a spouse or family member to your mortgage?

<!-- .al-answer -->
To put them on the loan, refinance with both of you as borrowers, so both apply and qualify on credit and income. To put them on title only, record a new deed. Under federal law, adding a spouse or child to the title of a home you live in generally does not let the lender call the loan due.

The [Garn-St Germain Act](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701j-3&num=0&edition=prelim) bars due-on-sale enforcement on a home with fewer than five units when the borrower's spouse or children become owners, when a divorce decree or settlement makes the spouse an owner, and on a transfer into a living trust that keeps the borrower as a beneficiary `[VERIFY: 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d)(6)-(8); 12 CFR 191.5 occupancy rule]`. It stops the loan from being called. It does not add or release a borrower.

If the person joining will share the costs, the questions match those in [co-buying a home with family or friends](/learn/co-buying-home-with-family-or-friends/).

### Does adding someone to the deed raise Michigan property taxes?

Not when an exemption applies. Adding a spouse is not a transfer of ownership, and since December 31, 2014 neither is deeding a home to a parent, sibling, child or grandchild. Creating a joint tenancy that keeps an original owner on title is also exempt. Adding anyone else as a tenant in common can partly uncap taxable value.

The rules are in [MCL 211.27a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-27a)(7)(a), (b), (i) and (u). The relative exemption covers your or your spouse's parent, sibling, child, adopted child or grandchild, only if the home is not used for a commercial purpose afterward, and the assessor can ask for proof within 30 days. The commission tells assessors to read exemptions strictly, so confirm with your city or township assessor before you record.

## How do you remove an ex-spouse from a mortgage in Michigan?

<!-- .al-answer -->
A quitclaim deed takes an ex-spouse off the title but not off the loan. To end their liability, the spouse keeping the home refinances in their own name, gets a lender-approved assumption with a release of liability, or sells. The judgment of divorce should say who keeps the home and by when the loan must be refinanced.

The lender is not a party to the divorce, so a judgment that assigns the mortgage to one spouse does not change who signed the note `[VERIFY: confirm with a Michigan family law attorney]`. Until a refinance or release, a late payment can affect both borrowers.

Michigan's tax rules turn on timing. A deed made as part of the divorce solely to end a tenancy by the entireties does not uncap taxable value. Former spouses who keep owning the home together become tenants in common, and a later transfer between them can partly uncap it, according to the commission.

If keeping the home does not work, compare [selling a home during a divorce](/learn/selling-home-during-divorce/). For a co-signer rather than an owner, see [refinancing to remove a co-signer](/learn/refinancing-to-remove-co-signer/).

### Can you take over the mortgage without refinancing after a divorce?

Sometimes, through an assumption. FHA, VA and USDA loans can generally be assumed with the servicer's approval when the person keeping the home qualifies, and the departing borrower should ask for a written release of liability. Most conventional loans cannot be assumed `[VERIFY: HUD 4000.1; 7 CFR 3555.256; Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac servicing guides]`.

On a VA loan, the veteran's entitlement can stay tied to the loan unless an eligible veteran who assumes it substitutes their own, which matters when the veteran is the one leaving ([38 U.S.C. 3714](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3714&num=0&edition=prelim)) `[VERIFY]`.

## How does a divorce buyout refinance work?

<!-- .al-answer -->
You refinance in your own name and use part of the new loan to pay your former spouse their share of the equity. Some loan programs treat a buyout under a divorce decree differently from a standard cash-out refinance. You need the decree or settlement agreement, an appraisal and enough income to qualify on your own.

Fannie Mae, for example, treats a buyout of a co-owner's equity under a written agreement as a limited cash-out refinance when the home has been jointly owned for at least 12 months ([Selling Guide B2-1.3-02](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.3-02/limited-cash-out-refinance-transactions)) `[VERIFY: current edition; HUD 4000.1 for FHA]`. For property tax, the commission says a judgment ordering one spouse to pay the other a set amount for the home is generally not a transfer of ownership, because it ends a tenancy by the entireties.

## What Michigan taxes and forms apply when a name changes on a deed?

<!-- .al-answer -->
A deed change can be a transfer of ownership that uncaps taxable value the next year, resetting it to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses. File a Property Transfer Affidavit with the assessor and keep the Principal Residence Exemption in place.

The year after a home sells, its taxable value resets to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid. A deed change that is not exempt triggers the same reset (MCL 211.27a(3)). In 2026, that reset raised a typical Michigan home's taxable value about 50.5% the year after a sale, according to Align Lending's [2026 Michigan Property Tax Uncapping Study](/research/michigan-property-tax-uncapping-study/). More on [how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale](/learn/michigan-taxable-value/).

1. **Property Transfer Affidavit ([Form 2766](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/treasury/Forms/Local-Government/2766.pdf)):** the new owner files it with the city or township assessor within 45 days (MCL 211.27a(10)). A late filing on a principal residence costs $5 a day, up to $200. File it and check the exemption box even when a transfer is exempt, so the home is not uncapped by mistake.
2. **Principal Residence Exemption ([Form 2368](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/Forms/Local-Government/2368.pdf)):** exempts the home you live in from up to 18 mills of school operating tax. A new owner who lives there files by June 1 for both levies or by November 1 for the winter levy. An owner who moves out files Form 2602 within 90 days. See [Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption](/learn/homestead-exemption-benefits-and-filing-guide/).
3. **Transfer tax:** state and county tax is not owed on a deed between spouses that creates or ends a tenancy by the entireties, a court-ordered transfer with no stated price, a joint tenancy with an existing owner or a deed for less than $100 ([MCL 207.505](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-207-505), [MCL 207.526](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-207-526)). Only the state tax exempts a deed to a child or grandchild. See [Michigan transfer tax](/learn/michigan-transfer-tax/).
4. **Recording fee:** charged by the county Register of Deeds `[VERIFY: current fee, MCL 600.2567]`.

A refinance by itself uncaps nothing and owes no transfer tax, because a mortgage is a security interest (MCL 211.27a(7)(j), MCL 207.526(d)).

<!-- .al-answer -->
Talk to a Michigan attorney before you record any deed during a divorce, an estate plan or a family transfer. The deed type, the form of ownership and the timing affect who is liable, what is taxed and who inherits. A mortgage broker can explain the loan side, not the legal one.

## Questions about changing names on a Michigan mortgage

### Can you add someone to a mortgage loan?

Yes, usually by refinancing. The person you add applies with you and qualifies on credit and income, and the new loan pays off the old one. Some FHA, VA and USDA loans can instead be assumed with the servicer's approval. Adding someone to the deed alone makes them an owner, not a borrower.

### Does a quitclaim deed remove you from the mortgage?

No. A quitclaim deed changes who owns the home, not who owes the loan. Everyone who signed the note stays liable until a refinance or sale pays it off, or the lender releases a borrower in writing. In a divorce, plan the deed and the refinance together.

### Can my ex keep the house and the mortgage in my name?

Yes, if the judgment allows it, but you stay liable to the lender. A late payment can affect your credit, and the debt can count against you on your next loan application. A judgment can set a deadline for your ex to refinance or sell, so ask your attorney what happens if it passes.

### Does adding a spouse to the deed uncap property taxes in Michigan?

No. Michigan law says a transfer from one spouse to the other, or a deed that only creates a tenancy by the entireties, is not a transfer of ownership, so taxable value stays capped. File a Property Transfer Affidavit and check the spouse exemption so the home is not uncapped by mistake.

### Can you assume a mortgage after a divorce?

Sometimes. FHA, VA and USDA loans can generally be assumed with the servicer's approval if the person keeping the home qualifies, while most conventional loans cannot. Ask for a written release of liability for the spouse who leaves. On a VA loan, the veteran's entitlement can stay tied to the loan.

### Do you need a refinance to add a spouse to a VA loan?

Not to add them to the deed. Federal law lets a borrower's spouse become an owner of a home the borrower lives in without the lender calling the loan due. To add a spouse to the loan itself, expect a refinance, and ask the lender which VA refinance options allow it.

### How long after a divorce do you have to refinance?

There is no general legal deadline. The judgment of divorce or settlement agreement sets one, if it sets one at all. The lender is not bound by the judgment, so both borrowers stay on the loan until a refinance closes. If you need time to qualify alone, raise it before the judgment is final.
