# MSHDA MI Home Loan: who qualifies, the limits and how to apply in Michigan

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 17, 2026

<div class="al-answer">

The MSHDA MI Home Loan is a fixed-rate first mortgage from the Michigan State Housing Development Authority (MSHDA) for first-time buyers anywhere in Michigan and repeat buyers in targeted areas. Three limits decide it: the 2026 sales price limit of $566,355, a county income limit and a 640 minimum credit score. You get it through an MSHDA participating lender.

</div>

<div class="al-takeaways">

**Key takeaways**

- The sales price limit is $566,355 statewide after June 1, 2026; older figures online are out of date.
- Income limits vary by county, household size and targeted-area status: $104,800 for 1 to 2 persons in most of Oakland County (June 1, 2026 chart).
- MI 10K adds up to $10,000 toward the down payment and closing costs, as a deferred loan, not a grant, and only with an MI Home Loan.
- MSHDA's $25,000 First-Generation down payment assistance is closed: funds exhausted, no waitlist (November 2026).

</div>

## What is the MSHDA MI Home Loan?

The MI Home Loan is a fixed-rate first mortgage run by the Michigan State Housing Development Authority, a state agency. It is a loan you repay, not a grant. MSHDA participating lenders originate it, and each one is underwritten as an FHA, VA, USDA Rural Development or conventional loan, so that loan type's rules apply too.

MSHDA sets the buyer, income, price and credit rules on its [MI Home Loan page](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/pathway-to-housing/mi-home-loan), and its [lender requirements page](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/lenders-realtors/mi-home-lender-requirements) lists the four loan types.

MI 10K, MSHDA's down payment help, is a separate second loan, covered below and in [Michigan down payment assistance options](/purchase/down-payment-assistance/). MSHDA's [Mortgage Credit Certificate](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/pathway-to-housing/mortgage-credit-certificate-program), applied for before you buy, is a tax credit of 20% of yearly mortgage interest ([IRS Form 8396](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8396)).

### Is MSHDA a lender?

Not one you apply to. MSHDA funds the program and sets its rules; a participating lender takes the application, underwrites it and closes the loan.

## Who qualifies for a MSHDA loan?

First-time buyers qualify anywhere in Michigan, and repeat buyers qualify only in MSHDA's targeted areas. Household income must be at or under the county limit, the price at or under $566,355 (2026), and the credit score at least 640. You must live in the home and complete a homebuyer education class.

### What counts as a first-time buyer for MSHDA?

Anyone who has not owned a home as a principal residence in the previous three years. Someone who sold five years ago counts again, and in a [targeted area](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/-/media/Project/Websites/mshda/homeownership/Homebuyers/Targeted-Areas_Flyer_050124-Final_accessible.pdf), past ownership does not rule you out.

### What is a targeted area?

A county, city or township where MSHDA accepts repeat buyers and sets higher income limits. On the June 1, 2026 chart, all of Washtenaw County is targeted, as are Pontiac, Royal Oak Township and Southfield, and Detroit, Dearborn and eight other Wayne County cities.

## What are the MSHDA income limits in 2026?

MSHDA sets MI Home Loan income limits by county and household size (1 to 2 persons, or 3 or more), with higher limits in targeted areas. The current chart is dated June 1, 2026. MSHDA replaces it when figures change, so check it before relying on any number, including those below.

The figures below are from MSHDA's [Income and Sales Price Limits chart for all 83 counties, June 1, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/-/media/Project/Websites/mshda/homeownership/Homebuyers/Income-and-Sales-Price-6126_accessible.pdf). They are household limits, so ask the lender whose income counts.

## What is the MI Home Loan sales price limit?

MSHDA's sales price limit for the MI Home Loan is $566,355 statewide after June 1, 2026. It caps the purchase price, not the loan amount, and it is the same in every county. Older limits still circulate online, including $544,233, the limit from May 1, 2025, and $224,500, the cap before that.

[House Bill 5032](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/about/press-releases/2024/12/20/passage-of-house-bill-5032-paving-the-way-for-expanded-homeownership-opportunities-in-michigan), passed in December 2024, replaced the flat $224,500 cap set in 2009 with an index: 90% of the IRS maximum sales price.

[Align Lending's Michigan Mortgage Market Report](/research/michigan-mortgage-market-report/) puts the median home value on 2025 purchase loans at $285,000 statewide, $385,000 in Oakland and $435,000 in Washtenaw, all well under the limit.

## How does MI 10K down payment assistance work with the MI Home Loan?

MI 10K is MSHDA's down payment assistance: up to $10,000 toward the down payment, closing costs and prepaid expenses. It is a deferred second loan with no monthly payment, repaid when you sell, refinance, pay off the first mortgage or stop living in the home. It must be paired with an MI Home Loan, and homebuyer education is required.

The [MI 10K page](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/pathway-to-housing/mi-10k-dpa-loan) lists these terms; MSHDA's lender page offers MI 10K with FHA, USDA and conventional MI Home Loans, not VA. <!-- [COMPLIANCE REVIEW] counsel item: MSHDA's page describes MI 10K as interest-free; this post leaves the interest terms out under F13. --> MI 10K replaced MSHDA's $7,500 statewide loan on May 8, 2023 ([May 12, 2023 release](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/about/press-releases/2023/05/12/mshda-expands-access-to-ten-thousand-dollar-down-payment-assistance-program)). Both programs use one [participating lender list](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/pathway-to-housing/lender-list-for-mi-10k-loan). Pairing MI 10K with a city, county or nonprofit program depends on both programs' rules and the order of the liens; see [Michigan first-time home buyer grants and programs](/learn/first-time-home-buyer-grants-and-programs/) for local options.

### Is the $25,000 First-Generation down payment assistance still available?

No. MSHDA's [First-Generation Down Payment Assistance](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/pathway-to-housing/firstgendpa) pilot is closed: its funds are exhausted and there is no waitlist. The $25,000 deferred loan launched in February 2025 with $8 million in state funding ([MSHDA release](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/about/press-releases/2025/02/18/new-mshda-program-offers-$25000-in-down-payment-assistance-to-aspiring-first-generation-homebuyers)).

## How do you apply for an MSHDA loan?

You apply through an MSHDA participating lender, not through MSHDA. The usual order: check the income and price limits, take a homebuyer education class, get preapproved with a participating lender, shop at or under the price limit, and then the lender reserves the loan and submits it to MSHDA.

1. **Check the limits** for your county, household size and price range.
2. **Take the homebuyer education class** listed in MSHDA's Housing Education Locator.
3. **Get preapproved with a participating lender** from MSHDA's lender list.
4. **Shop and write an offer under the limits,** with the lender's MSHDA steps built into your purchase agreement's dates.
5. **The lender reserves the loan and submits it to MSHDA.** MSHDA's commitment lasts 90 days on an existing home, 180 on new construction.

## When does an MSHDA loan not fit?

An MSHDA loan helps most when cash for the down payment is the barrier. For a buyer who already has the cash, it can cost more over time than an open-market loan. MI 10K comes due on a refinance or sale, and the income and price limits rule some buyers out. Compare Loan Estimates side by side.

**Three ways to finance a Michigan purchase** [COMPLIANCE REVIEW]

| | MI Home Loan + MI 10K | FHA on its own | Conventional with HomeReady |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who it fits | Buyers under MSHDA's limits | Buyers who meet HUD's rules | Buyers under the area income limit |
| Cash needed | MI 10K covers up to $10,000 toward down payment and closing costs | HUD's minimum down payment, set by credit score, plus closing costs | Fannie Mae's minimum down payment plus closing costs |
| Mortgage insurance | Follows the loan type underneath | Upfront and annual premiums; annual for the loan's life at the minimum down payment | Private mortgage insurance, removable as equity builds |
| On a refinance | MI 10K must be repaid | No second loan to repay | No second loan to repay |
| Income limit | MSHDA county chart | None | 80% of area median income |
| Price or loan limit | $566,355 sales price (2026) | FHA county loan limit | Conforming loan limit |

## What Michigan costs should you plan for with an MSHDA loan?

Plan for the property tax reset. The year after a home sells, its taxable value resets to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid. In 2026 that reset raised a typical Michigan home's taxable value about 50.5%. File your Principal Residence Exemption (Form 2368) by June 1 or November 1.

The 50.5% is from [Align Lending's 2026 uncapping study](/research/michigan-property-tax-uncapping-study/) (Michigan Treasury data); [MCL 211.27a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-27a) sets the reset. Budget for the new owner's tax. See [how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale](/learn/michigan-taxable-value/).

> **Hypothetical example, not an offer. Actual rates, costs and payments vary.**
> A home bought for $250,000, with an assessor's market value near the price, has a new taxable value of about $125,000 the year after the sale. At the 2025 statewide median homestead millage, 36.0 mills, the new owner's tax is about $125,000 × 36.0 ÷ 1,000 = $4,500 a year, about 1.8% of the price. That is tax only, not a loan payment; millage depends on the address.

The homestead millage assumes you claim [Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption](/learn/homestead-exemption-benefits-and-filing-guide/); the seller's does not carry over. File the affidavit with your city or township assessor (form on [Treasury's PRE page](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/property/principal-residence-exemption)), then [estimate your Michigan property tax after you buy](/michigan-property-tax-calculator/).

## Questions about MSHDA loans

### Who qualifies for a MSHDA loan?

First-time buyers statewide and repeat buyers in targeted areas, with household income at or under the county limit, a price at or under $566,355 (2026) and a credit score of 640 or more. You also need homebuyer education and a participating lender.

### Is MSHDA only for first-time home buyers?

No. First-time buyers, meaning anyone who has not owned a home in the previous three years, qualify statewide. Repeat buyers qualify only in targeted areas, which MSHDA's limits chart lists by county; all of Washtenaw County is targeted (June 1, 2026).

### How much is considered low income in Michigan?

It depends on the program and county. HUD defines a low-income family as one at or under 80% of area median income ([24 CFR 5.603](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-5.603)), and Fannie Mae's [HomeReady rule](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b5-6-01/homeready-mortgage-loan-and-borrower-eligibility) uses the same line. MSHDA's MI Home Loan chart is higher: $104,800 for one or two persons in most of Oakland County (June 1, 2026).

### What is the $25,000 down payment assistance program in Michigan?

It was MSHDA's First-Generation Down Payment Assistance, a $25,000 deferred loan for first-generation buyers launched in February 2025. It is closed: funds are exhausted and there is no waitlist. MSHDA's statewide help today is MI 10K, up to $10,000, paired with an MI Home Loan.

### What is the $7,500 first-time home buyer grant in Michigan?

Usually an older MSHDA amount. MSHDA's statewide down payment loan was $7,500 until May 2023, when it was retired and MI 10K opened statewide. Neither was a grant. MI 10K is up to $10,000, repaid when you sell, refinance, pay off the first mortgage or move out.

### Can you use an MSHDA loan with FHA, VA or USDA?

Yes. The MI Home Loan can be an FHA, VA, USDA Rural Development or conventional loan, so that loan type's rules apply as well as MSHDA's. MSHDA offers MI 10K only with the FHA, USDA and conventional versions. A USDA version still needs an eligible address and a household under USDA's income limit.

### Does the state of Michigan offer help with mortgage payments?

Not through a statewide fund right now. The Michigan Homeowner Assistance Fund, MSHDA's pandemic-era program, [stopped taking applications on December 8, 2023](https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/about/press-releases/2023/11/09/michigan-homeowner-assistance-fund-application-period-to-end-december-8-2023), once its federal funds were reserved. If you are behind on payments, call your servicer first, then a [HUD-approved housing counseling agency](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/find-a-housing-counselor/) to review your options.
