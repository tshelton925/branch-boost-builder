# Rent-to-own, land contract or mortgage: three ways to buy a Michigan home

Written by Samantha Shelton NMLS 1647301 · Updated November 16, 2026

<div class="al-answer">

Rent-to-own makes you a tenant with an option to buy. A Michigan land contract makes you the buyer, paying the seller instead of a bank. A mortgage makes you the owner, with a lender's lien. For a buyer weighing a seller's offer, three things differ most: who holds title, what a missed payment costs, and when your property tax resets.

</div>

<div class="al-takeaways">

**Key takeaways**

- **Title:** the owner keeps the deed under rent-to-own. A land contract gives you equitable title until payoff. A mortgage gives you the deed at closing.
- **Default:** eviction can start 7 days after a written rent demand. A land contract buyer gets 90 days or 6 months after a forfeiture judgment; a mortgage borrower, usually 6 months or 1 year after the sale.
- **Property tax:** taxable value uncaps the year after a land contract is signed or a home is sold. Rent-to-own usually waits for the deed.
- **The way out:** Fannie Mae limits rent credit to the gap between your rent and the appraiser's market rent.

</div>

## What is the difference between rent-to-own, a land contract and a mortgage?

With rent-to-own, the owner keeps title, you pay a landlord, and a missed payment can mean eviction and losing your option money. With a land contract, the seller keeps legal title, you pay the seller, and default can end in forfeiture. With a mortgage, you hold title, pay a lender, and risk foreclosure.

Your contract can change some of these Michigan rules.

| | Rent-to-own (lease-option) | Land contract | Mortgage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal title | Owner, until you buy | Seller, until payoff | You, from closing |
| Equitable title | Owner. You hold a lease and an option | You, the buyer | You. The lender holds a lien |
| Who you pay | Landlord: rent plus an option fee | Seller | Lender or servicer |
| Who pays property tax | Owner, unless the lease shifts it | Usually you, per the contract | You, often through escrow |
| Principal Residence Exemption | No, not as a tenant | Yes, with a written contract | Yes, for your principal residence |
| Taxable value uncaps | When the deed transfers, unless the lease has a bargain purchase option | Year after signing | Year after the sale |
| Lost on default | The home after eviction; fee and credits if the contract says so | The home and, usually, payments made | The home, after foreclosure and redemption |
| Path to a mortgage | Exercise the option, then a purchase loan | Pay off the contract, often by refinancing | Already there |
| Recorded? | Optional memorandum of option; ask an attorney | Recommended: contract or memorandum | Yes, at closing |

*Sources: MCL 211.27a, 211.7dd, 600.5714, 600.5744 and 600.3240; Michigan Department of Treasury.*

## How does rent-to-own work in Michigan?

Rent-to-own is a lease with a right to buy at a set price by a set date. You usually pay an option fee, and part of the rent may be credited toward the price. Not buying can mean losing the fee. A lease-purchase version can obligate you to buy.

A lease-option lets you walk away. A lease-purchase commits you, and the [FTC warns](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2016/11/what-you-need-know-about-rent-own-home-deals) that breaking it can bring legal action. `[VERIFY: FTC wording]` See [lease to own vs. rent to own](/learn/lease-to-own-vs-rent-to-own-whats/) for typical terms. Until you exercise the option, [Michigan Treasury](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/property/principal/principal-residence-exemption-pre-guidelines-and-faqs/ownership-requirement) treats the deal as a rental agreement, `[VERIFY: Treasury PRE FAQ]` so the [Truth in Renting Act](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-554-633) applies: the lease cannot waive the landlord's habitability duties or your right to notice and procedure in court.

## How does a land contract work in Michigan?

A land contract is a sale the seller finances. You pay the seller in installments, usually with a balloon payment due on a set date. You move in and usually pay the taxes and insurance, while the seller keeps legal title until the contract is paid off.

Michigan tax law counts someone "purchasing property under a land contract" as an owner ([MCL 211.7dd](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-7dd)), so the buyer can claim the Principal Residence Exemption under a written contract the seller signed. `[VERIFY: Treasury PRE FAQ]` See [how a Michigan land contract works](/learn/michigan-land-contract/) and [seller financing pros and cons](/learn/pros-and-cons-of-seller-financing/).

## What happens if you miss a payment on each one?

A rent-to-own tenant can be evicted and lose the option fee and credits. A land contract buyer faces forfeiture or foreclosure, with 90 days or 6 months to catch up after a forfeiture judgment. A mortgage borrower faces foreclosure, then usually 6 months or 1 year to redeem.

**Rent-to-own.** Eviction can start 7 days after a written demand for unpaid rent ([MCL 600.5714](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-5714)). After a judgment, the court waits 10 days before ordering you out, and paying the judgment and costs in that time stops it ([MCL 600.5744](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-5744)).

**Land contract.** Forfeiture works only if the contract provides for it ([MCL 600.5726](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-5726)). After a written notice, you have 15 days to pay or cure ([MCL 600.5728](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-5728)). After a judgment, the order to leave waits 90 days if you have paid under half the price, or 6 months if half or more. Pay and cure in that window and the contract continues; otherwise your right to redeem ends and your payments are usually lost. The seller can instead foreclose in circuit court ([MCL 600.3101](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-3101)).

**Mortgage.** A mortgage with a power of sale can be foreclosed by advertisement, outside court ([MCL 600.3201](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-3201)). For a home of up to 4 units, redemption lasts 6 months if more than two-thirds of the original loan was still owed, 1 year in most other cases, and 30 days if abandoned ([MCL 600.3240](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-3240)).

## How do property taxes work under each option in Michigan?

Under a land contract, the buyer usually pays the taxes, and taxable value uncaps the year after signing. Under rent-to-own, the owner pays until the deed transfers. With a mortgage, taxable value resets the year after the sale to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price.

A land contract counts as a transfer of ownership at signing, and taxable value does not reset again when the deed is recorded ([MCL 211.27a](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-27a)). A lease also counts if it runs over 35 years with renewals or has a "bargain purchase option", a price of no more than 80% of projected true cash value at lease end, so have your attorney check the option price.

In 2026 the reset raised a typical Michigan home's taxable value about 50.5% ([Align Lending's 2026 Michigan Property Tax Uncapping Study](/research/michigan-property-tax-uncapping-study/)). File a Property Transfer Affidavit within 45 days, then the Principal Residence Exemption by June 1 or November 1. See [how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale](/learn/michigan-taxable-value/).

## Do rent credits or land contract payments count toward a mortgage?

Rent credits count only under the lender's rules: Fannie Mae limits a credit to the difference between the rent you paid and the appraiser's market rent. Land contract payments build equity you can use when you refinance, and lenders ask for the contract and proof of every payment.

[Fannie Mae's Selling Guide B3-4.3-12](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-4.3-12/rent-related-credits) also wants a written lease with an original term of at least 12 months stating the rent and credit, and an appraisal showing market rent. `[VERIFY: B3-4.3-12, SEL-2024-05]` For a land contract signed more than 12 months before the application, Fannie Mae treats the payoff as a limited cash-out refinance valued on a new appraisal ([B2-1.3-05](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.3-05/payoff-installment-land-contract-requirements)). `[VERIFY: B2-1.3-05]` A seller's promise that "your rent goes toward the house" binds no lender.

## How do you move from rent-to-own or a land contract to a mortgage?

Start a lender review well before the option deadline or balloon date. Lenders look at your credit, income, payment history and an appraisal. A land contract payoff may be financed as a purchase or a refinance, depending on the program and when the contract was signed.

1. **Find the deadline:** the option or balloon date in the contract.
2. **Check your readiness early,** leaving time to work on credit, income documents or savings. If credit is the obstacle, see [credit repair steps before you apply](/learn/credit-repair-strategies-for-home-buyers/).
3. **Keep the paper trail:** the contract, proof of every payment, and the seller's written rent-credit or payoff statement. <!-- EDITOR: once /learn/how-to-get-pre-approved-for-a-mortgage/ is live, link "how to get preapproved for a mortgage" here. -->
4. **Apply, then order the appraisal and title work** in time to close before the deadline.

## What should you ask before you sign a rent-to-own or land contract deal?

Have a Michigan real estate attorney review the agreement first. Confirm the seller owns the home and whether a mortgage on it could be called due. Record the land contract or a memorandum of it. Get the price, credits, deadlines and default terms in writing.

1. Does a title search show the seller owns the home free of other claims?
2. Does the seller have a mortgage that this deal could let the lender call due? `[VERIFY: 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3]`
3. Will the contract or a memorandum be recorded with the county Register of Deeds?
4. Who pays for major repairs?
5. Who insures the home, and is your interest named?
6. When is the balloon or option deadline, and what if you cannot get a mortgage by then?
7. Is the price fixed now or set later?
8. How will option fees and rent credits be documented for a lender?

The [CFPB has warned](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/consumer-advisory-help-is-available-for-people-facing-housing-problems-because-of-a-contract-for-deed/) that contract-for-deed buyers can carry taxes, insurance and repairs without the deed, and the FTC warns of scammers advertising homes they do not own. `[VERIFY: CFPB page live; FTC wording]` Align is a mortgage broker, not a law firm, and this guide is not legal advice.

## Is renting, rent-to-own or a land contract cheaper than buying with a mortgage?

It depends on the price, the terms and how long you stay. Compare the full cost of each path, including option fees, the land contract's interest, taxes, insurance and repairs, against a mortgage you could qualify for, using your own numbers.

Three costs hide behind a monthly figure: an option fee you may not get back, a price set years ahead, and refinancing by the balloon date. The calculator above covers renting; [renting vs. buying, the financial comparison](/learn/renting-vs-buying-financial-comparison/) explains what to count.

## Questions about rent-to-own and land contracts in Michigan

### Is a land contract better than rent-to-own?

It depends on what you need. A land contract makes you the buyer: you hold equitable title and build equity, but carry taxes, repairs and a balloon date. A lease-option lets you walk away, usually losing the option fee. Have an attorney compare the actual contracts.

### Who pays property taxes on a land contract in Michigan?

Usually the buyer, because the contract says so. Taxable value uncaps for the year after the contract is signed, not when the deed is recorded at payoff. The buyer files a Property Transfer Affidavit within 45 days and can claim the Principal Residence Exemption.

### Can you claim the homestead exemption on a land contract in Michigan?

Yes. Michigan tax law counts a person purchasing property under a land contract as an owner for the Principal Residence Exemption, often called the homestead exemption. Use a written contract signed by the seller, and file the affidavit by June 1 or November 1.

### Do rent-to-own payments count toward a down payment?

Only part of them, and only under the lender's rules. Fannie Mae counts no more than the difference between the rent you paid and the appraiser's market rent, under a written lease-option with an original term of at least 12 months. Ask a lender to review it first.

### What happens at the end of a land contract if you cannot get a mortgage?

The balloon payment is still due. You can ask the seller for a written extension, sell and pay off the contract, or keep working toward a loan. If you miss it, forfeiture gives you 15 days after the notice, then 90 days or 6 months after a judgment.

### Is rent-to-own a good idea with bad credit?

It can buy time, but it costs money if the plan fails: the option fee and rent credits can be lost if no mortgage comes through by the deadline. Before you sign, find out how far your credit really is from a mortgage.

### Do you need a lawyer for a land contract in Michigan?

Yes, have one review it before you sign. A Michigan real estate attorney can check the title, the forfeiture and balloon terms and the seller's mortgage, and record the contract. Align is a mortgage broker and does not give legal advice.
