**Unique Michigan angle:** a deed change can be a **transfer of ownership that uncaps taxable value** unless an exemption applies; Michigan exempts transfers between spouses and, since 2014, certain transfers of a residence to relatives `[VERIFY: MCL 211.27a(7) exemptions, including (7)(s)]`. Getting this wrong can raise the tax bill by roughly half (Align's 2026 Uncapping Study: about 50.5% typical jump after a transfer).

## 3. Search intent and the reader's problem

Informational with a refinance decision behind it. Two readers: (1) someone who just married or wants to add a partner, parent or adult child to the home, and (2) someone divorcing who wants to keep the house and take the ex off the loan and the deed. Both are unsure whether they need a new loan, whether the lender can call the loan, what a quitclaim does, and whether taxes go up.

## 4. The angle that beats current results

1. **Loan vs. deed in one table:** what changes when you add someone to the title only, to the loan only, or both; who is liable; what the lender must approve.
2. **The federal due-on-sale exceptions** (Garn-St Germain) explained in plain English: transfers to a spouse or children, or by a divorce decree, generally cannot trigger the due-on-sale clause on a home you live in `[VERIFY: 12 U.S.C. 1701j-3(d)]`, but that does not remove the departing spouse from the loan.
3. **The three ways off a loan:** refinance in the remaining borrower's name, a lender-approved assumption with release of liability (FHA, VA and USDA loans are generally assumable with approval; most conventional loans are not `[VERIFY]`), or sale.
4. **The divorce equity buyout:** how lenders treat paying an ex their share (some programs treat a buyout under a divorce decree as a limited cash-out or rate-and-term refinance `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae B2-1.3-02 / HUD 4000.1]`), and what the decree must say.
5. **Michigan tax and filing checklist:** uncapping exemptions, the Property Transfer Affidavit (Form 2766), transfer tax exemptions for spouses and some family transfers `[VERIFY: MCL 207.505, 207.526]`, and keeping the Principal Residence Exemption (Form 2368, June 1 / November 1).

## 5. Outline

**Above the fold (≤60 words), must say:** adding or removing someone usually takes two separate steps, one for the loan (a refinance or an approved assumption) and one for the deed (a new deed recorded with the county); in Michigan, a deed change can uncap the home's taxable value unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses.

**Key takeaways (4):** loan and deed are separate · quitclaim does not remove anyone from the loan · Garn-St Germain protects certain family transfers from due-on-sale · check the Michigan uncapping exemptions before recording a deed.

**Module CTA after the loan-vs-deed table.**

### H2 Can you add someone to a mortgage without refinancing?
Answer (40–60 words) must say: usually not to the loan itself, because the original borrowers signed the note and the lender approved them; you can often add someone to the deed without changing the loan, but that makes them an owner, not a borrower, and the lender's consent and Michigan tax rules still matter.
- **Table:** Change · How it is done · Who is liable for the loan · Lender approval needed? · Michigan tax effect `[VERIFY]`. Rows: add to deed only · add to loan (refinance) · assumption by a new borrower · remove from deed (quitclaim) · remove from loan (refinance or release).

### H2 What is the difference between the mortgage and the deed?
Answer must say: the deed shows who owns the home and is recorded with the county Register of Deeds; the mortgage and note show who owes the money; a person can be on one and not the other, and each change needs its own paperwork.
- Link "conveyance and deeds in real estate" → `/learn/conveyance-in-real-estate/`.

### H2 How do you add a spouse or family member to your mortgage?
Answer must say: to put them on the loan, refinance with both of you as borrowers, which means both qualify on credit and income; to put them on title only, record a new deed; a spouse or child added to title on a home you live in generally does not trigger the due-on-sale clause under federal law `[VERIFY]`.
- H3 Does adding someone to the deed raise Michigan property taxes? Answer the exemptions; tell readers to confirm with the assessor before recording.

### H2 How do you remove an ex-spouse from a mortgage in Michigan?
Answer must say: a quitclaim deed removes them from title but not from the loan; to end their liability, refinance in your name alone, get a lender-approved assumption with a release of liability, or sell; the judgment of divorce should say who keeps the home and by when the loan must be refinanced.
- H3 Can you take over the mortgage without refinancing after a divorce? (assumption: FHA, VA, USDA with approval; most conventional loans not) `[VERIFY]`
- `[NEEDS SAMANTHA QUOTE: what she asks first when a client wants to keep the house after a divorce]`

### H2 How does a divorce buyout refinance work?
Answer must say: you refinance in your name and use part of the new loan to pay your former spouse their share of the equity; some programs treat a buyout under a divorce decree differently from a standard cash-out refinance; you need the decree, an appraisal and to qualify on your own income.
- Hypothetical example (F12 label): home value $300,000, existing loan $160,000, equity split 50/50 → buyout $70,000 → new loan about $230,000 before closing costs. Loan-amount arithmetic only; no rate, no payment. `[COMPLIANCE REVIEW]`
- Link "how a cash-out refinance works" → `/learn/what-is-cash-out-refinance-loan/`; money page "cash-out refinance in Michigan" → `/product-loans/cashout-loans/`.

### H2 What Michigan taxes and forms apply when a name changes on a deed?
Answer must say: a deed change can be a transfer of ownership that uncaps taxable value the next year, resetting it to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value (F16), unless an exemption applies, such as a transfer between spouses; file a Property Transfer Affidavit with the assessor and keep the Principal Residence Exemption in place.
- Checklist: Form 2766 within its filing window `[VERIFY]` · PRE Form 2368 if the owner occupying changes (June 1 / November 1, F5) · transfer tax exemptions · recording fees at the Register of Deeds.
- One sentence: "In 2026, the reset after a transfer raised a typical Michigan home's taxable value about 50.5% (Align Lending Uncapping Study)."
- Links: "how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale" → `/learn/michigan-taxable-value/`; "Michigan transfer tax" → `/learn/michigan-transfer-tax/`; "Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption" → `/learn/homestead-exemption-benefits-and-filing-guide/`.

### H2 What does it cost to add or remove a name?
Answer must say: a deed-only change costs the deed preparation and recording fees; a refinance has closing costs shown on a Loan Estimate; an assumption has the servicer's fees `[VERIFY: VA/FHA assumption fee caps]`; compare the total cost with the benefit before deciding.
- Links: "Review my break-even" → `/refinance/break-even-review/`; "refinancing closing costs" → `/learn/refinancing-closing-costs/`.

### H2 When should you talk to an attorney first?
Answer must say: before recording any deed during a divorce, an estate plan or a family transfer, because deed type, tenancy (Michigan recognizes tenancy by the entirety for married couples `[VERIFY]`) and timing affect liability, taxes and inheritance; a mortgage broker can explain the loan side, not the legal one.
