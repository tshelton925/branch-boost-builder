## 3. Search intent and the reader's problem

Informational, often first-time buyers. The reader receives SSDI, SSI, a private disability policy or VA disability and wants to know whether that income counts, how much house it supports, whether saving for a down payment will cost them SSI eligibility, and whether there are grants. Many have been told "disability income doesn't count", which is not true for most programs.

**Module CTA after the income H2.**

### H2 Can you get a mortgage on SSI or SSDI?
Answer (40–60 words) must say: yes; Social Security disability (SSDI), Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and other disability benefits can qualify as income for conventional, FHA, VA and USDA loans when you document them with an award letter and they are expected to continue; the loan still has to fit debt-to-income and credit rules.

### H2 How do lenders count disability income?
Answer must say: lenders use the monthly benefit on your award letter, may gross up non-taxable benefits by a set percentage under program rules, and generally do not need proof of continuance for Social Security disability unless the letter shows an end or review date `[VERIFY each]`.
- **Table:** Income type · Documentation · Continuance · Gross-up allowed? Rows: SSDI · SSI · VA disability compensation · private long-term disability · workers' compensation · SSI for a dependent child (if allowed `[VERIFY]`). `[VERIFY all rows: Fannie Mae B3-3.1-09; HUD 4000.1; VA M26-7]`
- Link "debt-to-income limits by loan type" → `/learn/debt-to-income-ratio-dti/`.

### H2 Will saving for a down payment affect your SSI?
Answer must say: SSI has a resource limit, so cash savings above it can affect eligibility; the home you live in does not count as a resource; an ABLE account, such as Michigan's MiABLE, lets eligible people save, and most of the balance does not count toward the SSI limit `[VERIFY: SSA limits and ABLE rules]`; check with SSA before moving money.
- Link "MiABLE" (Michigan Department of Treasury program page) and SSA's resource rules (external, Tier 1).
- SSDI note: SSDI has no asset limit `[VERIFY]`.

### H2 What loan programs work for buyers with disability income?
Answer must say: conventional, FHA, VA (for eligible veterans) and USDA (in eligible areas) all accept documented disability income; the right one depends on credit, cash for closing and the address; MSHDA's MI Home Loan can pair with some of them for first-time buyers, through participating lenders.
- MSHDA sentence with F2 wording; First-Generation DPA closed (F1).
- Links: (new, link once live) "MSHDA MI Home Loan" → `/learn/mshda-mi-home-loan/`; "USDA loan requirements" → `/learn/usda-loan-requirements/`; "VA benefits for disabled veterans in Michigan" → `/learn/va-benefits-disabled-veterans-michigan/`.

### H2 Are there housing grants for people with disabilities in Michigan?
Answer must say: there is no single federal grant to buy a home; help comes as down payment assistance from MSHDA and some cities, Housing Choice Voucher homeownership programs where offered, adapted-housing grants for eligible veterans, and nonprofit programs; each has its own income and eligibility rules `[VERIFY each]`.
- Link "Michigan down payment assistance options" → `/purchase/down-payment-assistance/`; "Michigan first-time homebuyer programs and grants" → `/learn/first-time-home-buyer-grants-and-programs/`.

### H2 Can a lender turn you down because your income comes from disability benefits?
Answer must say: no; the Equal Credit Opportunity Act and Regulation B bar discrimination because any part of income comes from a public assistance program, and the Fair Housing Act prohibits disability discrimination in housing; lenders may still verify the amount and that it will continue, like any income `[VERIFY]`.
- CFPB complaint link; "Align Lending is an Equal Housing Opportunity company."

### H2 What Michigan property tax help is available to homeowners with disabilities?
Answer must say: Michigan's homestead property tax credit has rules for people with disabilities, and low-income owners can apply to the local board of review for a poverty exemption; veterans with qualifying ratings may owe no property tax under MCL 211.7b `[VERIFY each]`.
- Links (new, link once live): "Michigan homestead property tax credit" → `/learn/michigan-homestead-property-tax-credit/`.

### H2 What should you look for in the home itself?
Answer must say: think about accessibility needs now and later (entry steps, door widths, a main-floor bedroom and bath), the cost of modifications, and whether renovation financing can include them.
- Links: "buying a home for aging in place" → `/learn/buying-home-for-aging-in-place/` (accessibility features); "renovation financing" → `/purchase/renovation-financing/`.
