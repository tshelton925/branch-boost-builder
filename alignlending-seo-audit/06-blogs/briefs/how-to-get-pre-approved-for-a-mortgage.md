## 3. Search intent and the reader's problem

Informational how-to with an action at the end. The reader is starting to look at homes (or an agent asked for a letter) and wants the exact steps, the documents to gather, what the credit check does to their score, how long it takes, and whether a letter from one lender locks them in. Michigan readers also need to know what changes in a Michigan offer: the mortgage contingency in the purchase agreement and a budget built on the new-owner property tax.

## 4. The angle that beats current results

National results (Rocket, NerdWallet, The Mortgage Reports) give generic steps and push an online application. We win with:
1. **A complete, checkable document list by borrower type** (W-2, self-employed, retired, gift funds) in one table.
2. **Plain truth about speed:** "instant" letters are prequalifications; a real preapproval needs documents reviewed. No time promises (F10).
3. **The credit pull explained with sources:** the hard inquiry, how multiple mortgage inquiries inside a shopping window are treated `[VERIFY: CFPB / FICO 45-day window]`, and that conventional credit is evaluated by Fannie Mae's DU without a fixed 620 floor for case files created on or after November 16, 2025 (F3).
4. **Michigan specifics:** a lender should qualify you with the **new-owner property tax**, not the seller's bill (Align's 2026 Uncapping Study: typical taxable value jump about 50.5% after a sale); the purchase agreement's financing contingency date; MSHDA buyers need a participating lender (F2); the letter's dollar amount should match the budget you are comfortable with, not the maximum.
5. **What can void a preapproval** after you have the letter, linking to the new "what not to tell a lender" post.

## 5. Outline

**Above the fold (≤60 words), must say:** to get preapproved you share your income, assets and debts with a lender, authorize a credit check, and provide documents the lender reviews before issuing a conditional letter; for Michigan buyers, make sure the lender figures your payment with the new-owner property tax, which can be well above the seller's bill.

**Key takeaways (4):** 6 steps · the documents list · the credit check and shopping window · letters typically expire and documents age (state as "lenders set an expiration, often tied to how old your credit report and pay stubs are" `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae document age rules]`).

### H2 What documents do you need to get preapproved?
Answer must say: most buyers need recent pay stubs, two years of W-2s, two months of bank statements, photo ID and details on debts; self-employed borrowers add two years of tax returns or, for bank statement loans, 12 to 24 months of statements `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae B3-3 / product page]`.
- **Table:** Document · W-2 employee · Self-employed · Retired · Gift funds. `[VERIFY each row against Fannie Mae Selling Guide document requirements]`
- Link "self-employed homebuyer plan" → `/purchase/self-employed-homebuyer/`.

### H2 Does getting preapproved hurt your credit?
Answer must say: a preapproval usually includes a hard credit inquiry, which can lower a score slightly; credit scoring models treat several mortgage inquiries within a short shopping window as one `[VERIFY: CFPB, window length by model]`; talking with a lender or broker before you apply does not require a credit pull.
- Link "what credit score you need" → `/learn/whats-good-credit-score-to-buy-house/`.

### H2 What credit score and income do you need to get preapproved?
Answer must say: there is no single number; FHA, VA, USDA and conventional programs each set or leave to lenders their minimums, and conventional credit is evaluated by Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter without a fixed 620 floor for case files created on or after November 16, 2025, though lenders may add overlays (F3); income matters through your debt-to-income ratio.
- Link "debt-to-income limits by loan type" → `/learn/debt-to-income-ratio-dti/`.
- Link (new, link once live) "income needed to buy a house in Michigan" → `/learn/income-needed-to-buy-a-house-michigan/`.

### H2 How long does preapproval take, and how long does a letter last?
Answer must say: once documents are in, a preapproval can take from the same day to a few business days depending on the file; letters carry an expiration set by the lender, and pay stubs, bank statements and the credit report must be current at closing `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae document age limits]`. No speed promise (F10).

### H2 Is there such a thing as instant mortgage preapproval?
Answer must say: an instant online result is a prequalification based on what you typed, not a review of your documents; sellers and agents in Michigan generally expect a preapproval backed by documents; use online tools to estimate, then get a reviewed preapproval before you offer.
- Link "prequalified vs. preapproved" → `/learn/prequalified-vs-preapproved-whats-difference/`.

### H2 Where should you get preapproved: a bank, a credit union or a broker?
Answer must say: no single place fits everyone (this section answers the "best place to get pre approved" query without the banned superlative); compare a bank, a credit union and an independent mortgage broker on the programs they can offer, the Loan Estimate, and how well they explain your options; a broker compares several wholesale lenders on one application.
- Broker statement (exact, from `/licensing/`).

### H2 What should Michigan buyers know about preapproval?
Answer must say: ask the lender to budget with the new-owner property tax, because the year after a sale taxable value resets to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price you paid (F16); Michigan purchase agreements set a date by which financing must be approved; MSHDA loans require a participating lender (F2).
- One sentence of Align data: 2026 typical reset about 50.5%; statewide median 36.0 homestead mills, about 1.8% of market value a year after the reset (F4).
- Link "estimate your Michigan property tax after you buy" → `/michigan-property-tax-calculator/`.
- Link (new, link once live) "MSHDA MI Home Loan" → `/learn/mshda-mi-home-loan/`.

### H2 What can void a preapproval after you have the letter?
Answer must say: new debt, a job change, large undocumented deposits, missed payments, or changes to the price or loan type can change or cancel a preapproval; tell your loan officer before making any financial change until closing.
