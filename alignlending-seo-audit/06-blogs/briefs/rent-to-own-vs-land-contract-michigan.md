## 3. Search intent and the reader's problem

Commercial investigation. The reader cannot get, or thinks they cannot get, a mortgage today (credit, self-employment, a recent move) and a seller or landlord has offered rent-to-own or a land contract. They want to know which option protects them, what they lose if it goes wrong, how taxes and title work in Michigan, and how long until they can switch to a regular mortgage.

## 4. The angle that beats current results

National articles treat rent-to-own and land contracts as generic "alternative financing". Michigan law makes them very different:
1. **Title and default:** under a Michigan land contract the buyer (vendee) holds equitable title and the seller must use forfeiture or foreclosure, with redemption periods set by statute `[VERIFY: MCL 600.5701 et seq. summary proceedings and redemption periods]`; under rent-to-own the buyer is a tenant with an option and can lose the option fee and rent credits.
2. **Michigan property tax:** signing a land contract is a transfer of ownership that can uncap taxable value, so the vendee should file a Property Transfer Affidavit and can claim the Principal Residence Exemption `[VERIFY: MCL 211.27a(6); Form 2766; PRE eligibility for land contract vendees]`; a rent-to-own tenant cannot claim the PRE.
3. **The mortgage exit:** what lenders want to see when you refinance a land contract or buy at the end of a lease-option (payment history, rent credits that may count only above market rent `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae Selling Guide rent-credit rule]`, appraisal, title).
4. A side-by-side table a reader can screenshot, plus a "questions to ask before you sign" list.
5. Clear routing to readiness: many readers could qualify for a mortgage sooner than they think.

### H2 How does rent-to-own work in Michigan?
Answer must say: you lease with an option to buy at a set price by a set date; you usually pay an option fee and sometimes rent credits; the option fee is often lost if you don't buy; lease-purchase versions can obligate you to buy.

### H2 How does a land contract work in Michigan?
Answer must say: the seller finances the sale; you make payments to the seller, usually with a balloon payment due after a few years; you take possession and pay taxes and insurance; the seller keeps legal title until the contract is paid off.
- Two sentences + link "how a Michigan land contract works" → `/learn/michigan-land-contract/`.

### H2 What happens if you miss a payment on each one?
Answer must say: a rent-to-own tenant can be evicted and lose the option fee and credits; a land contract buyer faces forfeiture or foreclosure with a statutory redemption period `[VERIFY: periods]`; a mortgage borrower faces foreclosure by advertisement with a redemption period, usually six months for most homes `[VERIFY: MCL 600.3240]`.

### H2 How do property taxes work under each option in Michigan?
Answer must say: under a land contract the buyer usually pays the taxes and the transfer can uncap taxable value the next year; under rent-to-own the owner pays and taxes uncap only when the deed transfers; with a mortgage, taxable value resets the year after the sale to the state equalized value, half of the assessor's market value, not automatically half the price (F16).
- One sentence with Align's figure: in 2026 the reset raised a typical home's taxable value about 50.5% (Align Lending Uncapping Study). File the PRE by June 1 or November 1 (F5).
- Link "how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale" → `/learn/michigan-taxable-value/`.

### H2 Do rent credits or land contract payments count toward a mortgage?
Answer must say: rent credits count toward a down payment only under specific lender rules, often just the portion above market rent and only with documentation; land contract payments build equity you can use when you refinance, and a clean 12-month payment history helps `[VERIFY: Fannie Mae and FHA rules]`.
