## 3. Search intent and the reader's problem

A veteran who already used a VA loan (still owns that home, sold it, or lost it to foreclosure) wants to buy again, or a veteran eyeing a higher-priced home wants to know whether a "VA loan limit" applies. They want to know how much entitlement remains, whether a down payment is needed, and how to restore entitlement.

## 5. Outline

**Above the fold (≤60 words), must say:** VA entitlement is the amount VA guarantees on your loan; with full entitlement there is no VA loan limit, and with entitlement still tied to another VA loan, the remaining guaranty is figured against your county's loan limit, which in Michigan is the same in every county ($832,750 for one unit in 2026, updated for 2027).

### H2 What is VA entitlement?
Answer must say: the dollar amount VA guarantees a lender, generally 25% of the loan; basic and bonus entitlement; why it matters for how much you can borrow without a down payment `[VERIFY]`.

### H2 Is there a VA loan limit in Michigan?
Answer must say: not with full entitlement; with remaining entitlement, the county limit (Michigan: the conforming limit in every county) sets how much VA will guarantee, and lenders may ask for a down payment so the guaranty plus down payment covers 25% of the loan.
- Link "Michigan loan limits by county" → `/loan-limits/` (owner of the figures).

### H2 Can you have two VA loans at once?
Answer must say: yes, if you have enough remaining entitlement; the second loan uses what is left, and a down payment may be needed for a larger price.

### H2 How do you restore VA entitlement?
Answer must say: sell the home and pay off the VA loan, or have another eligible veteran substitute entitlement on an assumption, or use a one-time restoration if you paid off the loan but kept the home; request it from VA `[VERIFY: VA Form 26-1880 process]`.

Answer must say: entitlement used on a foreclosed loan that caused a loss to VA is not restored until VA is repaid; remaining entitlement may still be available `[VERIFY]`.
