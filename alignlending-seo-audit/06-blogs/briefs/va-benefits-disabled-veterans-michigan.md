## 5. Outline

**Above the fold (≤60 words), must say:** two benefits stack: VA charges no funding fee to veterans receiving (or entitled to) compensation for a service-connected disability, and Michigan exempts the home of a veteran rated 100% permanently and totally disabled, individually unemployable or approved for specially adapted housing from property tax; both need paperwork.

**Key takeaways (4):** funding fee exemption rule · MCL 211.7b qualifying groups · Form 5107 with the local assessor · without the exemption, Michigan's post-sale reset raised a typical home's taxable value about 50.5% in 2026.

**Module CTA after the first H2.**

### H2 What VA home loan benefits do disabled veterans get?
Answer (40–60 words) must say: the same VA loan benefits as every eligible veteran (no monthly mortgage insurance, no VA loan limit with full entitlement) plus no funding fee for veterans receiving or entitled to service-connected disability compensation, and possible VA adapted-housing grants.
- Short table: Benefit · Who gets it · Where it is set (VA page). Rows: funding fee exemption, refund if rated after closing, adapted housing grants (SAH/SHA, amounts set yearly `[VERIFY]`), IRRRL and cash-out also fee-exempt.
- Link "VA loan benefits for every veteran" → `/learn/va-loan-benefits-for-veterans/`.

### H2 Who is exempt from the VA funding fee?
Answer must say: VA lists who is exempt: veterans receiving compensation for a service-connected disability or entitled to it, surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, service members with a proposed or memorandum rating before discharge, and active-duty Purple Heart recipients; the exemption shows on the Certificate of Eligibility.
- H3 Can you get the funding fee refunded if your rating comes later? `[VERIFY: VA refund rule and how to request]`
- Tool link: **Estimate my VA funding fee** → `/va-funding-fee-calculator/` (for readers who are not exempt or are checking).

### H2 Does Michigan exempt disabled veterans from property tax?
Answer must say: yes, under MCL 211.7b the homestead of a veteran with a qualifying service-connected disability is exempt from all property taxes; qualifying means 100% permanent and total disability, individual unemployability, or VA approval for specially adapted housing; an unremarried surviving spouse can keep it.
- H3 What does the exemption cover? (all property taxes on the homestead; not special assessments or water/sewer `[VERIFY]`)
- H3 Does a land contract or a home held in a trust qualify? `[VERIFY: Treasury guidance]`

### H2 How do you file Michigan's disabled veterans exemption?
Answer must say: file Form 5107 (Affidavit for Disabled Veterans Exemption) with the local assessor of the city or township, with the VA letter showing the qualifying rating; since 2025 it no longer has to be filed every year `[VERIFY]`; ask the assessor about the board of review timing for the current year `[VERIFY]`.
- Ordered list: get the VA benefit summary letter → complete Form 5107 → file with the assessor → keep proof → tell the servicer so escrow is recalculated. (Visible list; HowTo schema optional, see §10.)
- Link "Form 5107" → Michigan Treasury forms page.

### H2 How do these benefits change your loan and budget in Michigan?
Answer must say: the funding fee exemption lowers the loan amount or cash to close; the property tax exemption removes the tax line from the housing payment once granted, which can change your debt-to-income ratio; lenders may still escrow taxes until the exemption is on record `[VERIFY: lender practice]`.
- Hypothetical example (F12 label): a $280,000 purchase. Without the exemption, the new-owner tax at the statewide median 36.0 mills is about $5,040 a year (taxable value about $140,000 × 36.0 ÷ 1,000). With the exemption granted, that property tax line is $0. State only tax figures; no loan payment, no rate. No CTA in this section.
- Link "how Michigan taxable value resets after a sale" → `/learn/michigan-taxable-value/`; "estimate your Michigan property tax after you buy" → `/michigan-property-tax-calculator/`.

### H2 Do disabled veterans still need to meet VA credit and income rules?
Answer must say: yes; VA disability compensation counts as income (it is tax-free, and lenders may gross it up within program rules `[VERIFY: VA Lenders Handbook M26-7]`), and the loan still needs residual income and lender approval; the benefits change the cost, not the approval test.
- Link "VA loan requirements" → `/learn/how-to-qualify-for-va-loan/`.
- Link (new, link once live): "buying a home on disability income" → `/learn/buying-a-home-on-ssi-or-disability-income/`.

### H2 What if you do not qualify for the full Michigan exemption?
Answer must say: veterans below the 211.7b standard still get the Principal Residence Exemption like any owner-occupant, and some households qualify for the Michigan homestead property tax credit on the income tax return, which has rules for disabled filers `[VERIFY: MI-1040CR instructions]`.
- Links: "Michigan's Principal Residence Exemption" → `/learn/homestead-exemption-benefits-and-filing-guide/`; (new, link once live) "Michigan homestead property tax credit" → `/learn/michigan-homestead-property-tax-credit/`.

### H2 Where can Michigan veterans get help with the paperwork?
Answer must say: the Michigan Veteran Affairs Agency and county veteran service officers help with VA letters and benefit claims at no cost `[VERIFY: MVAA service officer locator]`; the local assessor handles Form 5107; a VA-accredited representative can help with ratings.
